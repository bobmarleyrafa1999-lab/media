## Caption

Rest and avoidance look identical from the outside. The difference is what you get up wanting.

Seneca was in favour of rest: he wrote that the mind comes back keener after a break. Avoidance doesn't give you that. It just moves the thing to tomorrow.

Which was your last evening?

Evidence: philosophy (Seneca, On Tranquillity of Mind 17), in our words.
