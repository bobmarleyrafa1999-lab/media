## Caption

Fast replies feel polite. Over time they teach people that your attention is always on tap.

Seneca made the point two thousand years ago: we're careful with money and careless with time. An hour's delay costs the message nothing. It might give you your evening back.

How fast do you usually reply?

Evidence: philosophy (Seneca, On the Shortness of Life 3), in our words.
