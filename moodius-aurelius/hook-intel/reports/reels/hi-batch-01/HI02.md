## Caption

A lot of us were told we were too sensitive before we had words for what we were noticing.

Sensitivity picks up real signals. The skill isn't feeling less. It's checking the signal before you act on it. Epictetus called it testing the impression.

Who told you, and were they right?

Evidence: philosophy (Epictetus, Enchiridion 1.5), in our words. Reflection: Moodius.

First comment: What does your sensitivity usually notice first?
