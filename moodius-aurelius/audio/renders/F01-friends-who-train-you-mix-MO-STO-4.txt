Seneca's advice isn't 'find a mentor'. It runs both ways: keep people who make you better, and be that person for someone else. You learn by helping.

In 2026 that might be the friend who asks how the thing you said you'd do is going. Or you being that friend.

Who made you better this year?

Source: Seneca, Moral Letters 7.8, tr. Gummere (public domain).
