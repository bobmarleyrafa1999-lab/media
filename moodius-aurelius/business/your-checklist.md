# Surafel's checklist: digital products

In order. The first block is what makes the journal purchasable.

## To sell the journal (about 1-2 hours of your time)

- [ ] **Read the journal** (`journal/out/The-Unexamined-Self-A5-digital.pdf`) and tell me anything to change. Edits go in `journal/content/the-unexamined-self.yaml` and I rebuild it.
- [ ] **Approve two costs**: a domain (about £10 a year) and a PO box or virtual address (about £5-15 a month). Tell me the domain you'd like.
- [ ] **Create a Cloudflare account** (free) and send me nothing; I'll give you the exact commands, or you approve Remote Control on your Mac and I run them.
- [ ] **Create a Stripe account**, stay in test mode, and apply for Managed Payments in the Dashboard. If Stripe says no, tell me and I switch to Lemon Squeezy (one setting).
- [ ] **Fill in your seller details**: the operator name shown on the legal pages (your name or a trading name), the PO box address, and a support email on the new domain.
- [ ] **Read the three legal pages** (privacy, terms, refunds; marked as drafts) and choose the refund window. It's set to 14 days.
- [ ] **Make one test purchase** with card 4242 4242 4242 4242 once it's deployed, and say "go live" when you're happy.

## To start the email funnel

- [ ] Decide the single list: Substack (recommended) or Kit. The storefront works with either.
- [ ] Create a Resend account (free) and verify the domain, so order emails actually send.
- [ ] Approve the welcome emails in `funnel/welcome-sequence.md`.
- [ ] Send me your Substack and Pinterest URLs.

## To promote it

- [ ] Pick which product reels to schedule (`funnel/reels/renders/`). Suggested start: P005 (free guide). They only make sense once the shop is live.
- [ ] Add saved replies in Instagram for JOURNAL and GUIDE with the links in `funnel/reel-concepts.md`, until the comment agent sends them.
