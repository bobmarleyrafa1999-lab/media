About

Stoicism for people who feel things

Moodius Aurelius is an anonymous writer and a small publishing project: essays, Instagram posts and reflection journals that put old philosophy next to modern psychology.

The Stoics are often sold as people who didn't feel anything. They weren't. Seneca wrote about the jolt of fear and the flush of anger; Marcus Aurelius wrote to himself on bad nights. What they offered was not numbness but a way to think clearly while feeling a lot. That's the version this project is about.

Our standards

  • Quotations are word for word from a named public-domain translation, or clearly labelled as our paraphrase. We cite book and section.
  • Every factual claim is tagged: philosophical argument, published research (with a note on how strong it is), or our opinion.
  • No invented studies, statistics or quotes. When a finding is small or contested, we say so.
  • No therapeutic promises. Reflection helps many people; it is not treatment.
  • Your data: as little as possible, never sold. See the privacy notice.
