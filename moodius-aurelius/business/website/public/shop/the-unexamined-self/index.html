The six parts

From noticing to living it

Part I · Days 1–5

Noticing

Watching your own mind, asking "what" instead of "why", and the blind spots only others can see.

Part II · Days 6–10

What Is Yours

The dichotomy of control, the story you add to events, distance, and rehearsing hard moments.

Part III · Days 11–15

Feeling Clearly

Naming feelings, finer emotional vocabulary, what feelings are for, anger and delay.

Part IV · Days 16–20

How You Attach

Your early map of closeness, moving toward or away, the conflict loop, asking plainly, repair.

Part V · Days 21–25

Who You Think You Are

The story you tell, roles and character, possible selves, approval, private and public selves.

Part VI · Days 26–30

What You Live By

Values versus goals, your calendar as evidence, the cost of a value, small commitments.

What do I get?

Two PDFs: an A5 digital edition you can type into on a tablet or computer, and an A4 edition for printing. Both have the same 30 days.

How is it delivered?

Can I use it in GoodNotes, Notability or on paper?

Yes. Import the digital PDF into any annotation app and write over it, type into the fields in a PDF reader, or print the A4 edition.
