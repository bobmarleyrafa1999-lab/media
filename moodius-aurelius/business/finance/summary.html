Merch

Print-on-demand means no stock. Etsy comes first because it collects UK and EU VAT for you. A tee keeps about as much per sale as a journal, but it needs samples (about £45), a second shop and physical returns, and people buy clothing from brands they already know. Launch after 50 journal sales and people asking for it. Until then the journal wins: it costs nothing to make and it already exists. Nothing has been listed. Choosing the supplier is up to you.

Brand deals

The rate card prices a deal from measured views only, at 2026 creator rates of $5–15 per 1,000 views. Today a reel is worth about £0.50–1.50 to a sponsor, so outreach isn't worth your time yet. At 1,000 followers and 1,000 views a reel, a reel is worth about £37 (the nano-tier floor). Good fits are stationery, books, education, mindfulness and professional development. Men's grooming is off-brand. Never: gambling, crypto, supplements or anything that makes health claims.
