# Appreciation angle, comments, and build-up arcs

Draft, 22 Oct 2026, from Surafel's notes: more history and psychology; don't come at everything from being hurt, look at life and appreciate things; more interaction through comments; short posts that build up to a bigger, researched post every so often (historical, teaching, philosophical, athletic, intellectual, artistic); abstract images, no faces unless he posts by hand.

## 1. Tone: appreciation first
- **At least two posts in three start from wonder, admiration, gratitude, curiosity or humour.** At most one in three starts from struggle or pain, and it still ends somewhere usable.
- **This doesn't cost reach.** The reel-first data says a sharp line about a person or a conflict wins the first 3 seconds. That works just as well with admiration: "This man taught himself maths from one borrowed book" is a person plus a gap. Curiosity gaps (Loewenstein, *Psychological Bulletin* 1994) don't need pain.
- **Psychology for this angle** (empirical tier; say "linked to", never "proven"):
  - awe: Keltner & Haidt 2003, *Cognition and Emotion*
  - gratitude journalling: Emmons & McCullough 2003, *JPSP*, "Counting blessings versus burdens"
  - savouring: Bryant & Veroff 2007, *Savoring*
  
  These go in the research library with their caveats before use.
- **Quick check before a hook ships:** would someone send this to a friend because it made them *feel more alive*, not only *less alone*? Both count, but the first should win twice as often.

## 2. Images: abstract, no faces
- Automated posts use places, water, light, stone, paper, handwriting, maps, tools and open-access art details. `fetch_archive.py` now drops anything titled as a portrait, crowd or person by default (9 tests). `--allow-people` exists only for posts Surafel makes by hand.
- **Pinterest:** Metricool shows Facebook, Instagram, Threads and LinkedIn connected on 22 Oct, but not Pinterest yet. Either way, pins are other people's work, so boards are a **mood reference**, not a source for images inside reels. The look gets matched with CC0 art (Met, Smithsonian, Rijksmuseum via Openverse), LoC "no known restrictions" photos of places and objects, and his own footage. Pinterest is still where our finished pins go once it's connected.

## 3. Comments and interaction
What works with the tools we have. Metricool can't read or answer comments, so replies stay with Surafel, or come from the community agent's draft queue once it's live.

| Mechanic | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| Easy-answer question | Every reel's last card + caption | One word, a number, or A/B ("Hokusai or Ramanujan?"). Never "comment YES": Meta's engagement-bait rules demote that on Facebook; treat Instagram the same |
| Moodius answers first | Pinned first comment | Moodius answers its own question in one line, so people see how to join in |
| You pick the next deep dive | Tuesday poll story (already in the plan) | Two subjects; the winner is named in the next teaser ("you picked…") |
| Guess the year | Quiz sticker on a teaser re-share | A fact from the teaser; answer revealed in the deep dive |
| What did I miss? | Last slide of every deep-dive carousel | The best addition gets quoted in the next post, credited, with the commenter's OK first |
| First hour | Surafel, by hand | Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting; a question back is better than a heart |
| Ask Moodius | Monthly question-box story | Questions answered as short reels |

## 4. Build-up arcs (short posts → a deep dive)
**Shape (every three weeks, agreed with the reel-first plan, no extra volume):** three short reels Thursday to Saturday marked "Part 1/2/3 of 3", then a 10-12 slide deep-dive carousel on Sunday at 12:00. Arc 1 is Epictetus: parts on Thu 12, Fri 13 (reel-first thread) and Sat 14 Nov (this thread, Discourses 4.1), and the deep dive on Sun 15 Nov (this thread, Meditations 1.7 + Discourses 1.18).

1. **Teaser 1, the mystery** (12-18 s): a striking fact, no name. "Who was this?" in the caption.
2. **Teaser 2, the turn** (12-18 s): the moment things changed; the name is revealed.
3. **Teaser 3, the lesson** (12-18 s): the Stoic or psychology link, plus the "deep dive Sunday" line and a quiz sticker on the story.
4. **Deep dive**: a 10-slide carousel (Sunday 12:00), a 45-60 s reel (engine format D, `max_s` 45-60), and, once Substack is live, the Thursday essay on the same person. Sources go in the caption.

Free Minds reels can be teasers inside an arc.
