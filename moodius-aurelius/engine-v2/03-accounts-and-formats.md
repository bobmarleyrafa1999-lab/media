## B. Creative principles to reuse (principles, never their scripts or images)
1. **One feeling, named exactly.** "Lonely" is generic; "lonely at your own birthday" is shareable. (1, 2, 3, 7)
2. **The scene comes before the idea.** Research lands after a moment the viewer recognises. (10, 12, 13)
3. **A word limit makes it read as true.** 100 words is a story; 12 words is a hook. (7)
4. **Silence in the image.** No people, lit windows, rain on glass: loneliness without saying "lonely". (23, 24)
5. **Hold the contradiction.** Name the paradox and don't resolve it too fast. (9, 18)
6. **Men speak to men as peers.** It's "talk to us", not "here's what's wrong with you". (14, 15)
7. **One number, made visible.** A single statistic carries a whole post. (16, and our own E18)
8. **Full attribution is a style.** Long, sourced excerpts can be beautiful. (5)
9. **Calm pacing beats cuts.** Slow pacing with one question per piece. (18, 19, 20)
10. **Critique the system, not the viewer.** Blame the feed, not the person scrolling it. (27)
