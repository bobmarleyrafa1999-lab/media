# 1. Editorial identity (21 Oct 2026)

**Moodius Aurelius is an anonymous digital journal about the contradictions of being human in 2026.** It sits where philosophy, psychology and the feeling of being a person meet, and it still runs on the Phase 1 positioning: *Stoicism for people who feel things.* This brief widens the range of sources (existentialism, absurdism, attachment and motivation research, bell hooks, Beauvoir) but keeps the visual system and citation rules as they are.

## The voice in one paragraph
It writes like a perceptive friend who reads too much: specific, slightly dry, never preachy. It names a situation before it names an idea. It's sure about what it has evidence for and open about what's opinion. It doesn't diagnose, doesn't sell a transformation, and never makes men feel better by putting anyone else down.

## Four labels on every idea
Every claim we publish carries one of these, in the research library (`02-research-library.csv`, column `evidence_tier`) and, where it matters, in the caption:

| Tier | What it is | How it shows on the post |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Empirical | Peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses, national surveys | Cite card: "Wildschut et al., J. Pers. Soc. Psychol. (2006)". No exaggeration of effect sizes. |
| 2 Philosophical | Primary texts and serious scholarship (SEP) | Public-domain translation and book + section for quotes. Modern authors are never quoted, only paraphrased: "Idea: Fromm, The Art of Loving (1956) · Moodius words". |
| 3 Opinion | Moodius's own reflection | Signed "— Moodius" and never dressed up as research. |
| 4 Anecdotal | Reddit, comments, cultural trends | Paraphrased, never identifiable. Used for language and hooks, never as proof. |

## Recommended improvements
1. **Put the evidence tier on the page, not just in our files.** A single small line in captions ("Study: …" or "Moodius reflection") is rare in this niche. It makes the account easier to trust and harder to copy.
2. **Make "Masculinity, Reconsidered" (E) a named weekly series.** It's the gap in the market: men's-wellbeing charities speak in campaigns, and the big philosophy pages either skip men's emotional lives or drift into toughness content. A calm, cited series fills that gap.
3. **Use the original names as series marks.** "Quiet Truths №12" and "Feel Log, 21 Oct" on covers make the archive feel like a journal and give returning viewers something to collect.
4. **Add a fourth texture without breaking the palette.** Fine film grain over Slate (now in the engine) plus your own phone footage or public-domain photographs at 72% Slate. This keeps "no statues, gold, marble or gradients" and still moves away from flat text.
5. **Ban list for masculinity content**, added to the engine's quality gate: alpha, sigma, high value, SMV, hypergamy, simp, beta, "female nature", red pill, plus the existing banned phrases.

## What stays exactly as it is
The palette (Slate, Graphite, Charcoal, Bone, Ash, Ember), Playfair + Inter, the 96px margin, the Ember accent used once, citations in public-domain translations, anonymity (no face, no voice, no identifying stories), one experiment at a time, and the cadence of 1 carousel + 1 reel + 2 stories a day until the 19 Nov review.
