Epictetus applies this to everything: a child, a wife, a farm. It sounds cold until you try it on a loss you're carrying.

'Lost' says something was taken from you. 'Restored' says you had it for a time. Both hurt. Only one lets you be grateful too.

What did you have, for a while, that you're still glad you had?

Quote: Epictetus, Enchiridion 11 (tr. George Long). Reflection: Moodius.
