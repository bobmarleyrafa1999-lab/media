## Formats that win in the niche
- **Numbered lists** ("3 Stoic rules for overthinkers") are the most-cited top format. ([flowshorts](https://flowshorts.app/instagram-reels-ideas/stoicism))
- **Can / can't control** two-column splits, insult responses, evening review, morning routine, memento mori. ([flowshorts](https://flowshorts.app/instagram-reels-ideas/stoicism))
- **Cite the source on screen** (book and chapter) for credibility.
- **Hooks decide everything.** A 90-day faceless self-improvement page went from <200 views per reel to 8-15k average once hooks got blunter ("You're not lazy. You're just distracted."). It posted 2 reels + 1 quote a day and reached ~18.7k followers in 90 days. Self-reported, treat as an anecdote. ([Medium](https://medium.com/@vireuoess/i-ran-a-faceless-instagram-page-for-90-days-d6b6ffc93b24))
- That page found reels **under ~15s** did best; our own "disrespect" reel kept viewers 17.3s of 19s. Keep reels 13-20s.

## Does posting 3 a day help?
Buffer's 2M-post study: going from 1-2 to 10+ feed posts a week lifts reach per post ~24% and follower growth ~5.5x (0.12% to 0.66% a week). The biggest jump is from 1-2 to 3-5 a week, and every step after that still adds. Observational, not causal. ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/))

## What we're changing
1. **3 posts a day** at 10:00, 12:00 and 18:00 Madrid (17:00 on Fridays), the top Metricool hours for our audience. Weekends use the same hours.
2. **Mostly reels.** Our week 1 reels got ~40x the views of image posts. Two of the three daily slots should always be reels.
3. **More list and contrast reels**, blunt "you" hooks, quote with chapter reference on screen, a save/send line at the end.
4. **Test a carousel** (list of 5 rules, one per slide) once a week for saves.
5. **No reposting** of other creators' clips, ever.
