# 2. The five strongest low-cost reel formats

Picked from the 10 in `01` for cost (text + flat Slate or one phone clip, no voice, no AI video), fit with "Stoicism for people who feel things", and evidence. All follow the Phase 1 design rules: Slate ground, Bone type, one Ember accent, Playfair + Inter, no statues, gold, marble or gradients. Code mapping to the strategy library is in the last column.

| Code | Format | Length | Structure (cards) | Judge it on | Strategy code |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | Quiet Truth | 12–18s | 2 hook lines → 1–2 body lines → one tiny action → CTA. No quote. | sends/reach | F9 as a reel |
| **R2** | Situation → old line | 16–20s | specific modern moment (hook) → the detail that makes it true → who said it → quote → book + section → one-line application → CTA | 3s hold, saves/reach | F1 / Phase 1 F2 |
| **R3** | Myth vs the text | 15–18s | the belief (hook) → turn → what the text or the psychology actually says → cite → reframe → CTA | comments/reach, sends | F2 / Phase 1 F10 |
| **R4** | Dry humour | 15–19s | "Me: …" or a short list → comic turn → the real point (line or study) → cite → CTA | sends/reach | F5 as a reel |
| **R5** | One book, one idea | 16–20s | a sharp claim (hook) → the book's idea in Moodius words → "Idea: Author, Book (year) · Moodius words" → the question to ask yourself → CTA | saves/reach | Phase 1 F3 |

## Rules that apply to all five
- **First 1–2 seconds:** the hook card is up from frame 1 (no logo intro), ≤ 12 words, names a specific situation, relationship or feeling. No "3 rules…", no "A reminder that…".
- **One idea per reel.** If a script needs "also", it's two reels.
- **Quotes:** verbatim from a public-domain translation (Long, Gummere, Trotter, Garnett, Common), followed by a cite card with book + section. Modern books (Frankl, Camus, Clear, Kishimi & Koga, Harris, Wiest, hooks, Fromm) are never quoted: their idea is put in Moodius words and labelled "Idea: … · Moodius words".
- **Visual treatments:** **V1** flat Slate (default, zero assets) or **V2** one original phone clip (no faces, no brands, locked-off) at 72% Slate tint. V2 only where the script names a footage tag.
- **Audio:** original ambient bed from `tools/ambient.py` (6 moods). `tools/score.py` cues remain available for the test arm. No voice-over, no trending audio by default (TikTok version can add one in-app).
- **Platforms:** the same MP4 goes to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (title = hook) and TikTok. Captions are written to work on all three.
- **Humour ratio:** at most 2 R4 reels in 7 until the data says otherwise.
