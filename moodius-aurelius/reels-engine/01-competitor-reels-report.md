# 1. Competitor intelligence for reels (20 Oct 2026)

Builds on `strategy/02-competitor-intelligence.md` (26 Stoic accounts, read this morning). This file adds 10 adjacent accounts and sources (psychology, men's mental health, literature, Reddit), new 2026 reel data, and the 10 most repeatable formats.

**Method and limits.** Instagram and TikTok block automated reading from here (robots.txt + proxy 403), so no posts were scraped and no per-reel hooks or retention curves were read directly. Figures come from public trackers and press, each dated. **V** = read from the named source on the date shown. **E** = estimate or inference. **D** = our own Metricool data. Per-reel numbers for competitors only become possible once they're added in Metricool (Analytics > Instagram > Competitors).

## C. The 10 most repeatable formats (and why they work)
| # | Format | Who shows it works | Why it works psychologically | Moodius version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Situation → old line**: name a specific modern moment, then a 2,000-year-old sentence about it | Everyday Stoic ratio (V), our hooks (D) | Recognition first ("that's me"), then the surprise that someone named it in 170 AD. Specificity signals the author understands you. | R2 |
| 2 | **Quiet Truth**: one or two lines that name a feeling, no lesson | Brianna Wiest 535k plays (V), stoicism.daily 7.49 likes/100 followers (V) | Being understood is the reward; sharing says "this is how I feel" without having to say it. Sends > likes. | R1 |
| 3 | **Myth vs the text**: "You've been told X. The source says Y." | TikTok Stoic format list (V, no numbers) | Correcting a belief creates an open loop; viewers stay to see if they were wrong. Comments rise because people argue. | R3 |
| 4 | **Dry humour**: list setup, comic turn, then the real point | Everyday Stoic 0.80 vs Daily Stoic 0.08 (V) | Laughing lowers defences, so the serious line after it lands. Humour is the most shared emotion in a solemn niche. | R4 |
| 5 | **Mechanism explainer**: "why you do X" | the.holistic.psychologist 1.87M avg views, Dr Julie (V) | Turns shame into a mechanism ("it's not you, it's how this works"), which relieves and gets saved. | R3/R5 |
| 6 | **One book, one idea**: a modern or classic book reduced to one usable idea | BookTok White Nights +463% (V), Pursuit of Wonder outliers (V) | Borrowed authority plus a shortcut ("I don't have to read it to get it"). Saves. | R5 |
| 7 | **Micro-story**: a real person's worst moment and what he wrote | The Stoic Mind 1.12M views/week (V, long-form) | Narrative transport holds attention longer than claims. Better at 25–40s. | F7 in strategy |
| 8 | **Thought experiment ending on a question** | FlowShorts philosophy shorts guide (V, no numbers) | Unresolved question pushes comments. | used in captions |
| 9 | **List / protocol** | Holiday 7.55M list post (V); our list reels 25–28% (D) | Lists promise completeness and get saved, but they hold badly as reels. | carousels only |
| 10 | **Reply to a comment** | TikTok-native (V, format exists) | Social proof and a real person's words; needs comments first. | later, once comments exist |
