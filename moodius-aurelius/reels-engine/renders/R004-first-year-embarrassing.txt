Nobody warns you that the early version of a better life looks a bit ridiculous from the outside. You'll be the one leaving at eleven, the one who doesn't drink, the one with the book.

Epictetus told his students to accept looking foolish about things that don't matter. The ones laughing are usually watching closely.

What's the habit you'd start if nobody could see?

— Moodius
