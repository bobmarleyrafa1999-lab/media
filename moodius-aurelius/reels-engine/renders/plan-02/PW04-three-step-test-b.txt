Part 3 of 3. Epictetus, Enchiridion 5 (tr. George Long): "It is the act of an ill-instructed man to blame others for his own bad condition; it is the act of one who has begun to be instructed, to lay the blame on himself; and of one whose instruction is completed, neither to blame another, nor himself."

Three steps. Most of us live on step one, visit step two on bad nights, and assume step three means not caring. It doesn't. It means the event happened, your opinion about it is yours to set, and there's no trial to hold. That's what a man who had been owned meant by free.
