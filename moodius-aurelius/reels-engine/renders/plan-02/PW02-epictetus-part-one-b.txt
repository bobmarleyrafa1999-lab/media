Part 1 of 3. He was born into slavery around AD 50 in Hierapolis, in what is now Turkey. Even the name we have for him, Epictetus, just means "acquired". His owner, Epaphroditus, was Nero's secretary, and while still enslaved he was allowed to study under Musonius Rufus, the leading Stoic teacher in Rome. He walked with a limp; ancient writers disagree about why.

Freed after Nero's death, he taught in Rome until Domitian banished philosophers from the city, then opened a school in Nicopolis, a small town in western Greece. Students travelled from across the empire to hear a former slave explain what is and isn't in your power.
