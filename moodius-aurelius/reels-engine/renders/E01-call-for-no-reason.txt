Men's friendships often run on excuses: the match, the stag do, helping someone move. When the excuses stop, the friendship goes quiet, and nobody decided that.

A 2017 meta-analysis found self-reliance was one of the masculine norms most consistently linked to worse mental health (Wong et al.). Being able to cope alone is good. Never letting anyone in isn't the same thing.

Who's the friend you keep meaning to text?
