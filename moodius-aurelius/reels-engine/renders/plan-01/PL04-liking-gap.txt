After a conversation with someone new, most of us think it went worse than it did. Psychologists call it the liking gap: in five-minute chats between strangers, people consistently underestimated how much the other person liked them. A study of college roommates found the gap lasted for months.

It's an average, not a promise. But it means the text you're not sending is probably more welcome than you think.
