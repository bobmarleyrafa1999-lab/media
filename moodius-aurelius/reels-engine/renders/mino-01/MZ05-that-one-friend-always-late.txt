Epictetus's two-word rule, as a Roman writer heard it second-hand: bear and forbear. Put up with what other people do, and hold yourself back from the things you'd regret.

Applied to the friend who's always late: bear the wait, skip the lecture, still order them a drink.

How late is your latest friend, in minutes?

Source: Aulus Gellius, Attic Nights 17.19 (J. C. Rolfe translation), reporting Favorinus on Epictetus.

— Moodius
