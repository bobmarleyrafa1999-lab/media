Musonius Rufus judged a lecture a failure if his students only praised it, and he tried to put new students off, as a test of who would come back.

One who came back had been enslaved in Rome and walked with a limp. His name was Epictetus, and nearly 2,000 years later people still read his notes.

Who was the teacher who never let you off easy?

Sources: Epictetus, Discourses 1.9, 3.6, 3.23 (George Long's translation, public domain).
