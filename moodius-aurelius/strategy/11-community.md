# 11. Community layer

Goal: turn viewers into people who talk back, then into people on the email list. Everything here is real conversation by the account owner. **No engagement pods, no bought engagement, no auto-DMs to strangers, no comment bots.** The Instagram API can't reply to comments or send DMs for us in a way that's allowed for this use, so those steps are yours; Claude writes the drafts.

## The ladder
| Step | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1. See | Carousel or reel reaches a non-follower (sends/reach) | Feed, Explore, Reels |
| 2. Speak | They answer the first-comment question or the 21:00 Journal story | Comments, story replies (DMs) |
| 3. Get answered | You reply within 60 min, in the Moodius voice | Comments, DMs |
| 4. Belong | They join the broadcast channel "The Journal" and see their answers featured | Instagram broadcast channel, stories |
| 5. Stay | They download the free guide and get the weekly letter | Email (Kit or Stan) |

## The voice (for every reply, DM and broadcast)
Calm, direct, warm. Name the feeling, then offer one move. One to three lines. Use their words back. About half of replies end with a question. Never argue; one calm line to a troll, or nothing. No "young men" framing, no lecturing, no emojis except the occasional 🙏 when thanking.

## 1. Comments (daily, you)
- **First-comment question:** Claude posts it with every post (it's in the scheduling manifest). It's a one-line, easy-to-answer question tied to the post, e.g. P1: "Which habit are you rebuilding trust with right now?"
- **Reply in the first 60 minutes** after the 12:00 and 18:00 posts. Use the bank below.
- **Pin** the best answer on each post (press and hold the comment > Pin).
- **Feature:** every Sunday, Claude picks 3 good answers from the week (you paste them, or Claude reads them from Metricool when available) and puts them on a story card "From the Journal" with first names or handles removed unless the person agrees.

## 2. DMs (daily, you)
- **Story replies** to the 21:00 Journal story arrive as DMs. Reply to every one the same evening or next morning, 1–2 lines.
- **People who send a post:** when someone comments "sent this to my brother/friend", DM a short thank-you: "Thanks for sharing it. That's how this grows. Anything you'd like a post on?" Only to people who engaged first; never cold DMs.
- **Ideas inbox:** answers to "Anything you'd like a post on?" go to Claude (paste them in this thread); Claude turns them into calendar rows.

## 3. Stories (Claude schedules, you add stickers twice a week)
- 09:00 Daily line and 21:00 Journal question are scheduled automatically every day. The Journal story ends "Reply to this story".
- **Tue and Sat (you, 2 min):** post one interactive story by hand from Claude's draft, because scheduled stories can't carry interactive stickers. Tue: poll ("Which is harder for you: anger or worry?"). Sat: question box ("What did you handle better this week?").
- **Sun:** "From the Journal" story featuring 3 answers (Claude makes it, you approve which answers).
- Add stories to highlights by the label printed on them (Journal stories go in **Journal**).

## 4. Broadcast channel "The Journal" (you create once, Claude drafts weekly)
- Create in the app: Inbox > Create (pencil icon) > **Create broadcast channel** > name "The Journal" > audience: followers. Then invite followers from the prompt Instagram shows, and add a story "Join The Journal" with the channel link sticker.
- Weekly rhythm, all drafted by Claude in the Friday report: **Mon** one line for the week + a poll; **Wed** a behind-the-post note ("why I made this one"); **Sat** the week's best answers and next week's theme. You paste them in (about 2 min each). Broadcast channels can't be posted to through the API.
- Only start it once you have about 100 followers; before that it's an empty room. Until then, the stories do this job.

## 5. Email list (the free guide)
- **The guide:** `lead-magnet/7-stoic-tools-for-loud-emotions.pdf` (10 pages, Phase 1 design, every quote cited). The old "7 ancient rules for young men" PDF is archived.
- **Tool:** Kit (free up to 10,000 subscribers; best for a weekly letter). Stan is the alternative if you also want a link-in-bio shop page.
- **Flow:** bio link → Kit landing page → email with the PDF → weekly letter every Sunday.
- **Welcome email (Claude's draft):**
  > Subject: Your 7 Stoic tools
  > Here's the guide: [link]. Read one tool a day for a week and do the practice. One request: reply and tell me which tool you're trying first. I read every reply. — Moodius
- **Weekly letter (Sunday, Claude drafts):** the week's best idea expanded to 300 words, one practice, one reader answer (with permission), next week's theme. You press send, or schedule it in Kit.
- Claude writes the landing page copy: headline "7 Stoic tools for when emotions are loud", sub "Regulate, decide and act. One tool a day for a week. Free.", one field (email), button "Send me the guide".

## 6. Measuring community (in the Friday report)
Comments per reach, story replies, DM conversations started, broadcast members, email subscribers and open rate. Target for the 30 days: every comment answered, ≥ 50 email subscribers.

## Claude does (automatic)
- Writes and schedules every first-comment question, the 09:00 and 21:00 stories, and the Sunday "From the Journal" story.
- Drafts the Tue/Sat sticker stories, the weekly broadcast posts, the welcome email, the Kit landing page and the Sunday letter, all in the Friday report.
- Updates the reply bank when new comment types appear, and turns follower ideas into calendar rows.
- Reports community numbers every Friday.

## You do (by hand)
- Reply to comments within 60 minutes of the 12:00 and 18:00 posts, and pin the best one (about 10 min a day).
- Reply to story replies and DMs (about 5 min a day).
- Post the Tue and Sat sticker stories (2 min each).
- Create the Kit account, upload the PDF, put the landing-page link in your bio, and send the Sunday letter.
- Create the broadcast channel at about 100 followers and paste in Claude's weekly posts.
