# Meme style and 20 original concepts

## The style in one line
Gentle, self-aware jokes about the gap between who we mean to be and what we actually do. The joke is always on us, never on a person or group.

## Yes
- Everyday contradictions (knowing vs doing, calm philosophy vs slow Wi-Fi).
- Philosophy applied to tiny problems. Marcus Aurelius vs the kettle.
- Reading, learning, gym habits, attention, procrastination, friendship, kindness.
- Visual jokes that work with no explanation: charts, flowcharts, Venn diagrams.
- Some memes are just funny. No lesson required.

## No (hard rules, never loosened by learning)
- Rage bait, politics, gender wars, religious mockery, humiliation, stereotypes, body shaming, sexual content, tragedy jokes.
- Health or psychology claims. Joke numbers are labelled "My numbers. Not science."
- Faces of real people, statues, gold, marble, third-party meme templates, brand logos, app screenshots, film stills.
- An engagement question or a paragraph of motivation under a meme.
- Reposting anything without written permission that covers commercial use and edits. Attribution is not permission. Watermarks stay.

## How a meme is checked
Text, image and context are judged together. The queue flags words linked to the excluded topics in the set-up, punchline, caption *and* the image description, and a flag always means "a person looks", never an automatic pass or fail. No filter guarantees every reader finds a joke fine, so anything ambiguous goes to you with the concern written out.

Scores (1–5) for humour, relevance and thoughtfulness only decide the review order. A 15/15 meme with no permission still can't be approved.

## The 20 concepts
All are `original`: our words, drawn in Pillow or set on a CC0 object photo with no people. Previews: `renders/<ID>.png`.

### MM01 · Mind · reading
**My to-read pile** / **is now load-bearing.**
- Visual: Typographic card. Optional photo version: a stack of books propping up a wobbly table leg (CC0 object photo, no people).
- Caption: "It's holding up the shelf. Emotionally, it's holding up me." #booklovers #tbr #reading
- Format: Single image (or 2-slide carousel: joke, then photo)
- Scores: humour 4, relevance 5, thought 2. Books pillar, instantly recognisable, sendable to any reader friend. No lesson needed.
- Concerns: None. Photo must show only objects.
- Reuse: original (cleared once approved)

### MM02 · Mind · knowing-vs-doing
**Knowing vs doing**: Podcasts about sleep I've finished 47, Nights I went to bed on time 2 (My numbers. Not science.)
- Visual: Two horizontal bars, Bone and Ember, on Slate. Small label: 'My numbers. Not science.'
- Caption: "Currently listening to number 48. In bed. At 1am." #sleep #habits #selfimprovement
- Format: Single image
- Scores: humour 4, relevance 5, thought 4. The gap between knowing and doing is the page's core idea, shown as a joke instead of a lecture.
- Concerns: Numbers are obviously personal and labelled 'not science', so they can't be read as a stat.
- Slide 2 footnote: Epictetus spent whole lectures on this gap: knowing the rule is not the same as living it.
- Reuse: original (cleared once approved)
