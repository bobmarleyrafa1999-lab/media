# Commercial approval cards

Every opportunity gets one card. You choose Approve, Reject or Request changes. Approving a brand doesn't approve its other products or future campaigns, and approving a card doesn't approve contacting anyone: each outward action (an email, a sign-up, a post) is a separate yes.
