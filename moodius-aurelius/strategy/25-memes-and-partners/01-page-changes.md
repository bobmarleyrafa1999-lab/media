# Page changes (subtle, in priority order)

What the data says first (Metricool, 6–26 Oct 2026):
- Carousels carry the account: 347 / 592 / 499 reach on 21–23 Oct. Reels sit at a median of 127 (18 reels).
- People barely share or save. Across the 5 carousels and 6 reels of 21–26 Oct: 8 saves and 5 shares in total. Sends are exactly what a good meme earns.
- The only meme so far (14 Oct, #youngmen, "Tag the friend…") reached 9 people. It was posted when the page had 3–4 followers, as a single image, with "young men" framing we've since dropped. That's a weak test, not proof memes fail.
- Audience: 86% UK (Metricool, read 27 Oct; the breakdown covers about 59 followers, so treat it as a hint).

## 1. Add a meme lane (biggest change)

- 3–4 of the 8 weekly feed slots from Mon 23 Nov, inside the approved standard week (no extra posts).
- Format: 2-slide carousel. Slide 1 is the joke. Slide 2 is the second beat of the joke, or a one-line source footnote when there's a real one. Never a lecture.
- One look: Slate background, Inter for the set-up, Playfair for the punchline, one Ember detail, small "moodius" mark. No third-party meme templates, no faces, no statues.
- Captions: one line that extends the joke + 3 specific hashtags. No question on memes (the brief says don't force it), so they're exempt from the closing-question rule.
