# Mino's formulas, rebuilt for a page that never shows a face

Each formula: what Mino does (label from `01-what-mino-teaches.md`), the Moodius version, the faceless visual kit, and the stop rule. Our own data still rules: carousels drive reach, reels sit at 106-211 reach, other-person openers hold best, "send this to the friend who…" beats "save this" (`strategy/19-reach-batch/01-reach-findings.md`).

**Faceless visual kit (all formulas):** text on Slate cards (reels engine), hands with a pocket book or notebook, a desk lamp or candle at night, a phone-chat mock-up, public-domain engravings and manuscripts (Wikimedia Commons, credited), owned b-roll of streets/trains/kitchens with no recognisable people. Optional voiceover: synthetic or none; never the owner's voice unless he chooses. No faces of anyone, no copyrighted film clips or music.

## F0. His two hooks, faceless
- **Mino (STATED, chapter titles):** Hook #1 "Is It Possible?" challenge; Hook #2 "Here's how to" + objection.
- **Moodius version:** "Is it possible to read a whole Stoic handbook in 7 days, 10 minutes a day?" (opens F1); "Here's how to calm a 2am spiral. No, not by 'thinking positive'." (objection named in the hook, answered by a sourced line).
- **Storytelling rules he lists:** one clear message, three acts, curiosity loops, sound design. Our engine already enforces one message per reel and a quiz-and-reveal loop; sound design = the ambient bed changing at the twist.

## F1. The Counter Serial ("day X of Y")
- **Mino (OBSERVED):** his four biggest TikToks in our sample are serial or arc posts with a day counter and a finish line ("day 340 of road to $100k a month", "AIRBNB ARC - day 1 of 5", then "the finale"). Series posts spike but don't raise his median.
- **Moodius version:** the follower is the experiment, not the creator. "Day 1 of 7: one line from a 1,900-year-old handbook. Today's job: …" Day 2 opens with the real reply count from day 1 ("[N] of you tried it"). Day 7 is a finale carousel. Proof comes from counted replies, never from an invented personal result.
- **Visual:** the same pocket book on the same desk every day, a hand-drawn tally that grows. Counter big in the top third.
- **Stop rule:** if days 1-3 median reach is below our reel median (≈150), finish the 7 (a promise is a promise) and don't start another serial before the 19 Nov-style review.

## F2. The Story Arc (mission → stakes → twist → callback)
- **Mino (OBSERVED by Cre8):** origin hook, mission as a question, conventional-wisdom stakes, a self-deprecating joke, a progress number, a cliffhanger, a twist with a music change, a callback ending.
- **Moodius version:** a historical person's story told in 7-9 cards. Mission = what they wanted; stakes = what everyone believed; twist = the sourced surprise; callback = the hook line returns with a new meaning. The joke is ours (dry, kind, never at the subject's expense).
- **Visual:** public-domain engraving or map for the person, then text cards; one pattern change (colour flip Slate → Bone) at the twist.
- **Stop rule:** keep if 3-second hold beats 40% on 2 of 3; drop if all three are under 30%.

## F3. Result First (result hook + proof)
- **Mino (third-party tag, Keepylabs):** "Result hook · Story · Personal proof."
- **Moodius version:** the result belongs to the source, not to us. Open on the outcome from the record ("A thief stole his lamp. He thanked him."), then show how it happened, then the line it left us. No "this changed my life" claims.
- **Visual:** object b-roll (lamp, key, coin) + text.

## F4. Pep Talk vs The Old Version (his "slop vs viral" split)
- **Mino (OBSERVED, owner's screenshot):** a split-screen reel labelled SLOP vs VIRAL comparing two versions of a video.
- **Moodius version:** left half "the pep-talk version" (a generic slogan, clearly labelled as generic), right half "the 1,900-year-old version" (a sourced line, in Moodius words if paraphrased). End on "A or B?" so comments carry the answer.
- **Visual:** split screen, left in Ash on Graphite (dull), right in Bone with an Ember rule. This is the one format where Remotion's split layout helps; the current engine can fake it with two stacked cards.

## F5. That One Friend (his early relatable sketch)
- **Mino (OBSERVED):** his older channel's best shorts were "That One Friend Who…" sketches.
- **Moodius version:** a chat mock-up or a two-line sketch of a recognisable friend, then a sourced line that names why that friend matters. Lean positive: the friend who does favours and never mentions them, the friend who always pays, the friend who texts "home safe?". Ends with "Send this to that friend."
- **Visual:** phone-chat mock-up rendered from text (no real chats, no real names), then cards.

## F6. Countdown to a free thing (his launch countdowns)
- **Mino (OBSERVED):** "35 days until launch… 22 days until launch" before each product.
- **Moodius version, only if the owner says yes to the free read-along (see `03-ecosystem.md`):** "7 days until the free Enchiridion read-along starts." Each day gives one useful line now, so the countdown is never just a sales post.

## Not copied
- Money-counter hooks ("$242k / $10m"), income screenshots, "generational wealth" framing: wrong brand, and Moodius has no income story to show.
- "Post 30 times a day" (his X snippet): our own data says 3 reels a day wasted effort.
- Face-to-camera confidence challenges: anonymous brand.
