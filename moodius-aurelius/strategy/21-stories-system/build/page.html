Three things to know

Stickers

No scheduler can add a poll, quiz, slider or question sticker. That's Meta's rule. So each interactive story comes twice: a scheduled version that asks for a typed reply, and a sticker version you post from your phone in about a minute. Posting by hand is optional, and test X11 checks whether it's worth the minute.

Scoreboard

Metricool's story analytics come back empty, and Meta's API reports 0 replies for accounts in Europe. Replies get counted in the app and your DMs. The tracker spreadsheet is built around that: about 10 minutes on Sundays.

What stories are for

Instagram's head says feed posts reach new people and stories reach "your most passionate audience". Carousels stay the growth engine. Stories turn viewers into people who reply, and replies move you up their story bar.
