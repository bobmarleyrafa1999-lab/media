# Making the account conversational

The goal: Moodius has considered views, says them plainly, and genuinely wants to hear back. Followers feel answered, not marketed at.

## The seven formats

| Format | How it runs | Your input | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **An uncomfortable question** | 21:00 private-reflection hook ("Don't answer, just notice…") → anonymous question box → 09:00 Moodius take | None, or swap in your own take | 1 a week (Monday) |
| **Two perspectives, one dilemma** | Real-life dilemma, both sides fair → poll → 09:00 both sides spelled out + where Moodius lands | Optional: your view | 1 a week |
| **What psychology actually says** | Guess → answer with study + caveat ("small study", "linked, not cause") | None (all sourced) | 2 a week |
| **A question worth asking yourself** | One question, question box, Moodius answers first | Your one-line answer | 1 a week |
| **Books that changed how I think** | Audience names books (question box) → next morning, answers shared + your pick | **Only books you've read** | 2 a month |
| **A stranger's act of kindness** | Question box collects real stories → 3 shared next morning (question-box answers show no names) | Pick which 3 to share | 2 a month |
| **The argument against my own opinion** | My view vs the strongest case against it → poll "who wins?" → question box "change my mind" → where I've landed | **Your real view** | 2 a month |

Plus the recurring ones already running: quiz → answer (most nights), Friday "you pick", Monday "about you", Add Yours twice a month.

## How you add your own perspective (5 minutes a week)

Keep a note on your phone called **Moodius takes**. Once a week, write three lines for any topic coming up in the calendar:

```
Topic: (e.g. closure)
Where I've landed: one sentence.
Best argument against me: one sentence, said fairly.
What would change my mind: one sentence.
```

Paste it into the thread, or edit the "Moodius take" frame text yourself. Every take in the sequences is a draft in the page's voice: if it isn't what you think, change it or cut it. Never post a take you don't hold.

Voice check before posting:
1. Would I say this to a friend across a table? (If it sounds like a poster, rewrite.)
2. Did I give the other side its best version, not a straw man?
3. Is there a small, true, slightly odd detail? (Your weird-but-friendly humour lives here.)
4. Does it end on something they can answer in one line?

## Sharing what followers send: rules

- **Question-box answers**: Instagram shows the answer without the sender's name when you share it. You can share these. Still skip anything that could identify someone (names, places, workplaces).
- **DMs and story replies**: never screenshot or share without asking first ("Could I share this anonymously?"), and only if they say yes in writing.
- **Never** invent a follower message, a testimonial, a "someone asked me…" that nobody asked, or a reply from a fake account. If a frame needs real answers and you don't have enough, post the scheduled version instead (the sequences have one).
- Crisis or self-harm messages: see the response bank, section "Someone is struggling". Don't share, don't debate.
