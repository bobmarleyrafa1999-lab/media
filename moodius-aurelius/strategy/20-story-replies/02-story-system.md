# The story loop (from 26 Oct 2026)

## Daily

| Time (Madrid) | Story | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 21:00 | **Question**: hook line + A/B or one-word reply | Gets the reply (DM) |
| 09:00 next day | **Answer** + "full story at 12:00" | Pays off, sends people to the carousel |
| 12:00 | Carousel on the same story | Reach |

Two stories a day, no more. Answers go in a **Quizzes** highlight on Sundays (see 03-page-refocus.md).

## Weekly shape

- Mon 09:00: "about you" check-in (self-disclosure).
- Fri 21:00: "you pick" vote (A history / B mind / C kindness). The winner gets one extra carousel the next week.
- Every other night: a guess-the-answer quiz tied to the next day's carousel.

## Optional real stickers (by hand, ~1 minute)

Automatic stories can't carry stickers. On nights you feel like it: save that night's question image from renders/, post it from the Instagram app at 21:05, and add a Quiz sticker with the same A/B options (mark the right answer from the 09:00 card). Skipping it breaks nothing.
