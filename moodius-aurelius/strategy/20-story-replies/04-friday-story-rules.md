# Friday build: story rules (from 25 Oct 2026)

Add to the weekly routine after the reach-batch addendum (step 9). Evidence: 01-research-sweep.md.

10. **Stories are a question-answer loop.** 21:00 = question with a one-word or A/B reply, tied to the next day's 12:00 carousel. 09:00 = the answer with a source line and the footer "… at 12:00". Two stories a day only. Render with tools/stills_v3.py kind "story" (see strategy/20-story-replies/build/stories.py).
11. **No stickers in Metricool** (API can't). Never write "tap the poll". Ask "Reply A or B".
12. **Monday 09:00** = an "about you" question with Moodius's light answer first. **Friday 21:00** = "you pick" vote (history / mind / kindness); give the winner one extra carousel next week, if he reports the result.
13. **No new quotes in stories.** Copy only quotes already verified in that week's carousels. Facts carry the source on the card; medical history carries "History, not medical advice."
14. **Bait check on every CTA:** one ask; sends name a recipient; never "tag 3 friends", "comment YES", "like if".
15. **Wrong-sounding answer is right about half the time**, so guesses feel like a game, not a test.
