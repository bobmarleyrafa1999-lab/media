## The one thing to know

Instagram does not let any scheduler (Metricool included) put a poll, quiz or question sticker on a story. So the automatic stories ask for a **one-word reply** instead ("Reply A or B"). Replies land in DMs, and Instagram says story replies are one of the signals it uses to decide whose stories people see first. Real stickers are optional: post the same image from your phone and add the sticker, about one minute each.

## Rules respected

Anonymous (no faces, no personal details), no gendered framing, every fact sourced in the card, no new quotes in stories (the two quotes shown are copied from carousels already checked), every story ends on a short question, £0 spend.
