# Moodius Aurelius: Phase 1 Summary
20 October 2026

Full playbook: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ffe5bf92-34bd-4d74-a107-b90837ad82ba

## Positioning
**Stoicism for people who feel things.** Modern Stoic practice explained through psychology: how to regulate, decide and act when emotions are loud.

Why: the loudest Stoic content online skews toward aggression and hyper-masculinity, while accounts like The Everyday Stoic (~500K, over 75% female audience) prove demand for emotionally literate Stoicism. The name Moodius fits this gap.

## Brand audit verdicts
- Name: keep.
- Pillars: keep all seven; give relationships and emotional maturity (pillar 6) more weight.
- Highlights: keep all seven, reorder (Manifesto, Daily, Tools, Discipline, Truths, Journal, Identity) with plain one-word cover labels.
- Visual identity: the biggest gap. Build two Canva templates and six components first.
- Cadence: one carousel plus one reel per day for 30 days, then let the data decide.

## Two rules from day one
1. Every quote cites book and section (e.g. Meditations 4.7) in a public-domain translation (Long for Marcus and Epictetus, Gummere for Seneca). Paraphrases are labelled "Moodius".
2. No AI statues, gold, marble or gradients. Instagram has signalled a preference for human-feeling content in 2026; a recognisable design system is the hedge.

## What the research says (verified)
- Instagram's top ranking signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers.
- Carousels have the highest engagement rate of any format (0.55%) and drive the most saves. Single images are the weakest format and still declining.
- About 80% of carousel engagement is attributed to the cover slide. A swipe cue lifts engagement slightly.
- Short, loopable reels are rewarded because watch time counts replays.

## Competitor tiers
- Author networks (Daily Stoic 3.5M, Daily Philosopher, Daily Dad): daily cadence, one idea per post, one source piece repurposed everywhere. Proof that one operating model runs several brands.
- Product-led anonymous brands (Stoic Reflections 1M, Stoicismideas 200K+): monetise without a face via shop or programme. This is the model to copy.
- Quote aggregators (174K to 195K range): avoid. They plateau.

## Design system (proposed)
- Canvas 1080 x 1350 carousels, 1080 x 1920 reels, 96 px safe margins, 12-column grid.
- Palette: Slate #1B1F24, Graphite #2A2F36, Charcoal #3A4049, Bone #F3EFE7, Ash #B8B3A9, Ember #C9742E (single accent).
- Type: high-contrast serif for covers and quotes (Playfair or Cormorant), humanist sans for body (Inter, Source Sans or Figtree), small-caps citations bottom left.
- Six components: claim cover, pull quote, term card, numbered step, pair slide, sign-off.
