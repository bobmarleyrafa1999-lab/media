**About following accounts.** Following a page helps you research it and lets its owner notice you. There's no evidence that following anyone makes Instagram show your posts to that account's followers, and Instagram's own description of ranking (watch time, likes and sends per reach) doesn't include it. Never use follow/unfollow to get noticed.

**Reach, conversion and retention are separate jobs.**
- *Reaching new people*: carousels, reels, comments on other pages, and later collabs. Measure non-follower reach and shares.
- *Converting profile visitors*: the name, bio, pins and top tiles. Measure follows per 1,000 reach.
- *Keeping followers*: replies, stories, a consistent promise. Measure repeat commenters, story replies, and followers lost.

For curious adults who like books and ideas, feel things deeply, and are tired of hustle slogans and "alpha" motivation. Warm, curious and hopeful in tone, careful about evidence. (Audience age and location are a hypothesis: Instagram shows demographics only once an account has enough followers.)

The "when someone is rude to you" opener, which won your best reach so far, is a hook pattern you can use inside series 4 and 5. It isn't a seventh series.

### Rules each series follows

**Books (series 1).** Every post is labelled either *Researched introduction* or *Moodius review (read it)*. Only the second is a personal review, and only when you've told me you read the book and what you thought. Never write "I read this" or invent your reaction. Quote briefly, name the author, work, translation or edition, and comment on it in your own words. Modern books are paraphrased and labelled; short quotations come only from editions we can check.

**Quotations (all series).** Check every quotation against the text: Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg, MIT Classics, or the publisher's edition. Name the translator. If wording can't be confirmed, paraphrase and label it "Moodius paraphrase". Watch the common fakes (for example, "We are what we repeatedly do" is Will Durant summarising Aristotle, not Aristotle).

**Films (series 2).** Original analysis only. Name the film, year and director. Don't use clips, stills, posters or soundtrack. Attribution, short length or editing don't make a copyrighted clip safe to use, and Instagram now removes repost-heavy accounts from recommendations (see platform facts). Public-domain films are fine, but check their status for the UK and US separately.

**Religion and spirituality (series 4).** Name the specific tradition and text, for example "Theravada Buddhism, Karaniya Metta Sutta (Sutta Nipata 1.8)" or "Zhuangzi, chapter 3, tr. James Legge". Keep three things apart on the slide: what the tradition teaches, how people interpret it, and any empirical claim (with its own source). Never line up quotes from different religions as if they say the same thing.

**Health and neuroscience (series 3, 6).** Use systematic reviews and large studies first. Say whether the evidence comes from humans, animals or cells, and whether it's an experiment or an observed link. State uncertainty in plain words. Traditional remedies are history unless clinical trials support a claim, and every medical-history post carries: *Historical interest only, not medical advice. Some traditional remedies are toxic.* Instagram also keeps exaggerated health claims out of recommendations, so careful wording protects reach too.

## 5. The measurement system

### The tracker

`tracker.csv` (one row per post or story sequence) and `tracker.xlsx` (the same columns, formulas filled in). Fill it in during the Sunday review. Metricool supplies most columns; the ones marked *app* only exist in the Instagram app (Insights on the post).

| Column | From | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| date, slot, pillar, series, format, hook, opener_tone, cta, experiment, arm | You / the build | opener_tone: curiosity, appreciation, tension, hurt |
| your_minutes | You | Your time only |
| reach, views, likes, comments, saves, shares | Metricool | Per post |
| non_follower_reach_pct | App | Post insights → Accounts reached → Followers / Non-followers |
| avg_watch_s, hold_3s_pct | Metricool (reels) | Instagram shows hold as "skip rate" in the app; hold = 100 − skip |
| follows | Metricool (feed posts) / App (reels) | The API gives follows for feed posts only |
| profile_visits | App | Not available from Metricool |
| story_reach_first, story_reach_last, story_replies, sticker_taps | App | Story insights disappear after 24 h unless saved; story replies read 0 through the API for European accounts |

### Formulas

| Measure | Formula | What it tells you | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shares per 1,000 reach | shares ÷ reach × 1000 | Whether people pass it on. Instagram says sends matter most for reaching non-followers | Tiny numbers (0–2 a post) swing wildly. Use medians across 5+ posts |
| Saves per 1,000 reach | saves ÷ reach × 1000 | Whether it's useful enough to keep | Same small-number problem |
| Likes per 1,000 reach | likes ÷ reach × 1000 | Approval from people already connected | Followers like more than strangers, so it mixes audiences |
| Follows per 1,000 reach (post) | follows ÷ reach × 1000 | Conversion from that post | Feed posts only via the API; reels in the app; Instagram counts only follows from that post's screen |
| Follows per 1,000 reach (account, weekly) | (followers at end − followers at start) ÷ weekly account reach × 1000 | Overall conversion | Net figure: unfollows are subtracted, and it includes follows from anywhere |
| Profile visit rate | profile_visits ÷ reach | How often a post makes someone curious | App only |
| Visit-to-follow | follows ÷ profile_visits | How well the profile converts | App only; small numbers |
| Non-follower share | non-follower reach ÷ reach | Whether a post is finding new people | App only, rounded percentages |
| 3-second hold | hold_3s_pct | Whether the hook works | Reels only |
| Story completion | story_reach_last ÷ story_reach_first | Whether people stay to the end | Story reach counts unique viewers per frame |
| Story replies per 1,000 | story_replies ÷ story_reach_first × 1000 | Conversation | Count in the app; the API reports 0 for Europe |

**What Instagram doesn't tell you.** It doesn't say which post a follower came from when they followed from your profile, doesn't credit reach from shared DMs to the original post reliably (22 Oct: 5,734 account reach vs about 700 credited to posts), and doesn't give reel follows through the API. Don't fill those gaps with guesses. Leave the cell empty.

### Two experiments

One main variable each. Compare medians across several comparable posts. No winner from one viral post.

**E1 · Carousel opener tone** (already running on the scheduled posts, 26 Oct–19 Nov)
- Question: do carousels that open with curiosity or appreciation get shared as much as ones that open with tension or hurt?
- Arm A (tension/hurt): 26, 27, 29, 30 Oct, then alternate days 5–19 Nov.
- Arm B (curiosity/appreciation): 28, 31 Oct, 1, 2, 3, 4 Nov, then alternate days 5–19 Nov.
- Kept the same: carousel, 12:00, same design system, a share-style CTA.
- Primary metric: median shares per 1,000 reach. Secondary: median reach, follows per 1,000 reach.
- Minimum: 8 posts per arm. Arm B gets its first six in a row, so dates are confounded until the alternating days balance it.
- Call at the 19 Nov review: one arm leads only if its primary median is at least 30% higher and its reach median isn't more than 30% lower. Otherwise it's "no clear winner", and the brand preference for a hopeful tone decides.

**E2 · Reel hook type** (the planned X5 test, 2–19 Nov, 18:00 reels, alternate days)
- Arm A: situation hook ("Phil Connors lives the same day…"). Arm B: curiosity hook ("There's a scene in Groundhog Day that explains…"). Every new reel above carries both.
- Kept the same: 18:00 slot, 30–35 s, engine template, closing 1–3 word question.
- Primary metric: median 3-second hold. Secondary: average watch time, sends per 1,000 reach.
- Minimum: 8 reels per arm. Call at the 19 Nov review on the same 30% rule.

E1 replaces the cover-style test X1 for carousels, so only one variable changes per format at a time.

### When to repeat, revise or stop a series

Judge each series after five posts, against the account's own 30-day medians for that format.

- **Repeat**: median reach and median shares per 1,000 reach both at or above the account median. Give it more slots.
- **Revise**: one of the two below median. Change one thing (hook, length, visual) for the next three posts.
- **Stop**: both below median after five posts plus three revised ones. Park it for 60 days.
- With under five posts, the call is "too early". A single post far above the rest is a lead to repeat, not a verdict.

## Platform facts vs my hypotheses

### Verified from Instagram or primary reporting (checked 25 Oct 2026)

- Adam Mosseri, January 2025: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." Likes count slightly more for followers, sends slightly more for non-followers. Track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), [original post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/)
- Recommendations favour original content without watermarks. Engagement bait, low-quality repurposed content and exaggerated health claims aren't recommended. Accounts that mostly repost others' content are excluded from recommendations. [Instagram Creators, 30 Apr 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality)
- Reels: vertical 9:16, hook in the first 3 seconds, keep text from covering most of the screen, 3 minutes or less to be recommended. Keywords in your name and bio help search. Check Account Status for eligibility. [Instagram Creators, Aug 2022, updated Apr 2023](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- Trial reels go to non-followers first; results show after about 24 hours. [Instagram Creators, 10 Dec 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Hashtags are capped at five per post, rolling out from December 2025. Mosseri says hashtags don't increase reach but do label a topic. [Social Media Today, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- The repost policy was extended to photos and carousels, judged across an account's posts over a month. [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
- From earlier work on this project: story replies read 0 through the API for European accounts, and the API gives follows and profile visits for feed posts and stories, not reels.

### My hypotheses (to be tested, not facts)

- Carousels with a tension opener and a kind payoff are this account's best reach format.
- Hopeful and curious openers will be shared at least as much as hurt ones (E1 tests this).
- Five carousels a week will beat your three-reel baseline for reach per hour of your time.
- "Send this to the friend who…" helps when it's natural. Used on every post, it starts to look like the engagement bait Instagram demotes. Use it on about half the posts, and vary the wording.
- Collaborations will pay off only after relationships built through comments.
- Following accounts doesn't change distribution. There's no evidence it does.
