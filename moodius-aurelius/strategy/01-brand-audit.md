# 1. Brand audit (Phase 1 verdicts, approved 20 Oct 2026)

## Positioning
**Stoicism for people who feel things.** Modern Stoic practice explained through psychology: how to regulate, decide and act when emotions are loud.
Why (from the Phase 1 Summary): the loudest Stoic content skews aggressive and hyper-masculine; emotionally literate Stoicism is the gap, and the name Moodius fits it. This replaces the 13 Oct "young men" framing in all new captions.

## Verdicts
| Area | Verdict |

## Two rules from day one
1. Every quote cites book and section (e.g. Meditations 4.7) in a public-domain translation (Long for Marcus and Epictetus, Gummere for Seneca). Paraphrases are labelled "Moodius paraphrase".
2. No AI statues, gold, marble or gradients.
