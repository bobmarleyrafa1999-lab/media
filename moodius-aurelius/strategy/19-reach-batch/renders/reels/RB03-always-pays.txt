Everyone has the friend who grabs the bill before you've found your wallet. Turns out they might be getting something out of it.

One small brain-scan study (19 people, Science 2007) found that giving to charity lit up reward areas, even when the gift was compulsory, and more when it was chosen. A Gallup-based study across 136 countries linked spending on others to more wellbeing in 120 of them. Linked, not proven to cause it.
