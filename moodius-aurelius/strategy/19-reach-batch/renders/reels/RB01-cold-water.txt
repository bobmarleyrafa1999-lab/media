In the 1970s Tu Youyou's team was hunting a malaria drug and their extracts kept failing. She went back to a 4th-century first-aid handbook by Ge Hong, which said to soak sweet wormwood in water and drink the juice. It never said boil. Heat had been destroying the active compound. Cold extraction worked, and artemisinin became a frontline malaria drug. Nobel Prize, 2015.

Tag the friend who never reads the instructions. Tu Youyou read them twice. 😅

Historical interest only, not medical advice. Modern artemisinin is purified and dosed; wormwood juice is not a treatment. Some traditional remedies are toxic.
