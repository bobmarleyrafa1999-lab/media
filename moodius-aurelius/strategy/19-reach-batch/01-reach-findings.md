# What gets @moodiusaurelius reach (25 Oct 2026)

Sources: Metricool API pull on 25 Oct 2026 (brand 7224060, all posts 6-24 Oct, account daily totals), the Instagram feed export of 24 Oct (strategy/18-tone-rebalance/data/), strategy/16-lessons-2026-10-22.md, hook-intel/, Words That Hold (PR #10), Tone Audit (PR #11). Raw rows: data/per-post-2026-10-25.csv and data/account-daily-2026-10-25.csv.

Small account, small numbers: 25 posts, 59 followers. Read every rule below as "the strongest lead we have", not a law. Re-check at the 19 Nov review.

## The plain-words version

1. **Carousels are what moved the account.** Before 21 Oct the account reached 100-230 people a day. The day the first carousel went out it reached 2,141; the next day 5,734; then 2,398. Followers went 7 → 14 → 51 → 57 → 59 in those four days. Nothing else changed that week except carousels starting (and Metricool going paid).
2. **Reels have a ceiling right now.** Every reel since 6 Oct landed between 106 and 211 reach, whatever its hold rate. Good hooks raise watch time, not reach. Reels are worth keeping for testing hooks, but carousels are the reach engine for now.
3. **The opener decides it.** Top three carousels: someone being rude to you (585), the 2am replay (473), the self-trust reframe (342, and the only post Instagram credits with follows: 5). The calm "here's a routine" carousel got 6. Tension in the first line wins; tips and routines don't.
4. **Shares are the lever.** The best post had the most shares (2) and used "Send this to someone who deals with people all day." Posts asking you to send to a specific kind of person beat "save this" every time.
5. **Dead formats, confirmed again:** quote cards (4-5 reach), memes (9), the 08:00/10:00 slots (20-32 reach), pep talks ("Motivation isn't coming", 20), numbered-list openers (hold only 18-30% at 3 seconds).
6. **Hold rate leaders (reels):** "You can't make everyone like you" 46.8%, "You didn't choose your family" 46.1%, the anger reel 45.5%, "Not every insult deserves a reply" 43.1%. All open on another person. The two-branch "if it's true… if it's false…" reel held people 16.5 seconds, four times any other.
7. **Positive topics haven't been tested fairly.** 100% of posts so far are Stoic Human Mind, mostly hurt. The one "humour + tag a friend" post was a meme image (a dead format), so it tells us nothing about humour itself.

## Gaps the data can't close

- On 22 Oct the account reached 5,734 people, but the per-post numbers add up to about 700. Instagram's API doesn't credit most of that day to one post (shares into DMs, Explore and profile visits aren't split out), and it reports no follows at all for reels. The Instagram app's own "Top content" view would show it. Optional for Surafel: a screenshot of Professional dashboard → Accounts reached → Top content (last 30 days).
- Story reach comes back empty from the API, so stories can't be judged yet.

## What this batch does with it

| Rule from the data | How the batch uses it |
|---|---|
| Carousels drive reach | 6 carousels in the batch, at 12:00 |
| Tension in line one | Every hook opens on a person, a contradiction or a puzzle, then pays off in kindness, discovery or a laugh |
| Shares beat saves | Every post ends with "Tag/send this to the friend who…" naming a specific type of friend |
| Other-person openers hold best | Most hooks name another person (a student, a goat volunteer, the friend who always pays) |
| Two-branch structure holds 16.5s | Reels use a quiz card ("Hot water or cold?") then the reveal |
| Positive untested | 2 Forgotten Knowledge, 2 Good Life, 1 Reading Room style story, Human Mind with a kind payoff |
| No quote cards, memes, lists, pep talks, 08:00/10:00 | None used |
