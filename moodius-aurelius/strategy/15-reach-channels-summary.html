Where to buy reach, and where to earn it

Paid ads at our size buy information, not profit. The biggest free lever is still posting the same reels on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Two small paid tests are ready and wait for your OK.

The answer

  1. Connect TikTok and YouTube Shorts first. Same reels, twice the surface, €0. I schedule them once they show up in Metricool.

  2. No paid channel pays for itself yet. A guide sign-up is worth about £0.20 and a journal sale leaves £10.77. Every benchmark puts the cost above that.

  3. Run two small learning tests when their gates are met: €35 on an Instagram reel to the free guide, and about €18 of TikTok Promote for followers.

  4. Skip for now: Google Search (a sale costs ≈£21-77 in clicks), TikTok Ads Manager ($500 minimum), Substack Boost (paid-subscription discounts only).

  5. New organic places: Tiny Buddha, The Good Men Project, the Stoic Coffee Break podcast, an r/Journaling giveaway, Substack recommendation swaps.
