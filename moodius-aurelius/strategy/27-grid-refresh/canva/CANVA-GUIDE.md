# Making grid-refresh posts in Canva

Four ready-made covers are already in your Canva account (Projects > Designs). Each is an editable
1080 x 1440 post (Instagram's 3:4 portrait, which is exactly what the grid shows, so nothing gets cut off).

| Colour | Canva design | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Slate | Moodius post template v2 (grid refresh) | All options, steps 1 to 3 |
| Bone | Moodius cover v2 · Bone | Options A and B |
| Sand | Moodius cover v2 · Sand | Option C |
| Olive | Moodius cover v2 · Olive | Option C |

## One-time fixes (2 minutes)

1. Open each design, click the big headline, then **Effects** on the top bar, then **None**.
   (Canva added a faint shadow behind the headline; the brand look has no shadow.)
2. Optional: add the brand colours to your palette. Home screen > **More** > **Brand** > **Colors** >
   **Add palette**. The free plan allows three colours; use Slate `#1B1F24`, Bone `#F3EFE7`, Ember `#C9742E`.
   (Brand fonts need a paid plan; the templates already carry the right fonts, so you don't need them.)

## Making a new post

1. Open the cover in the colour the plan says (see `PLAN` below), then **File** > **Make a copy**.
2. Double-click the headline and type the new hook. Keep it to 4 lines or fewer.
3. Change the small label at the top: MIND, BODY or PEOPLE, then the post number (`MIND · NO. 42`).
4. Move the orange underline under the one or two words that matter most. Drag its ends to fit the
   words; never underline a whole line.
5. **Share** > **Download** > PNG. Upload in Metricool as usual.

## Rules that keep it professional

- One underline per post. One accent colour (Ember) per post.
- No photos, emojis, stickers or extra shapes on the cover. Inner slides can carry a chart or a quote.
- Headline left-aligned, never centred. Leave the empty space under it empty.
- Fonts: Playfair Display (headline), Inter (everything else). Never mix in a third font.
