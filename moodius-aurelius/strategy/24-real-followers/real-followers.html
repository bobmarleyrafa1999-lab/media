1. Spot-check the newest followers Today · 10 min

  1. Open Instagram, tap your profile picture (bottom right), then tap 146 followers.
  2. Tap Sort (top right of the list) and pick Date followed: Latest.
  3. Look at the top 60 or so (everyone from 25-26 Oct). Tap any that look odd to open their profile.
  4. For a bot, tap Remove next to it in your followers list, then Remove again. They are not told.
  5. If it's posting scams or pretending to be someone, open its profile, tap ⋯ (top right) and then Report.

Probably a bot (two or more of these)

  • No profile photo, or a model or crypto stock photo
  • Username of a name plus a long string of numbers
  • 0-3 posts, following thousands, followed by few
  • Bio selling something: "DM for promo", crypto, forex, "earn £…"
  • Has already sent you a DM with a link or a "collab" offer

Keep (looks like a quiet real person)

  • Private account with a normal name and a photo
  • Few posts but a believable follow count
  • Follows other book, philosophy or wellbeing pages
  • Liked or saved one of your carousels

Remove in small batches (20-30 at a sitting). Mass-removing in one go can look like automation to Instagram. If it's under a handful, just remove them and move on. If it's dozens, tell me and I'll note the date so the weekly numbers stay honest.

2. Switch on the spam filters Today · 10 min

All of these live under one menu. In the app: Profile → ☰ (top right). Then scroll to the section How others can interact with you.

a. Hidden Words (catches spam comments and DMs)

  1. Tap Hidden Words.
  2. Under Offensive words and phrases, turn on Hide comments, Advanced comment filtering and Hide message requests.
  3. Under Custom words and phrases, tap Manage custom words and phrases, paste the list below, tap Add.
  4. Turn on Hide comments and Hide message requests for the custom list too.

Custom list (copy all of it; commas are fine):

promo, promote it, promotion, collab?, DM us, send it to @, check my bio, link in bio, bio link, follow back, f4f, follow for follow, l4l, crypto, bitcoin, btc, forex, trading signals, passive income, earn £, earn $, cash app, giveaway winner, you won, verify your account, copyright violation, meta support, blue tick

These words never appear in your own replies or in the "Reply A or B" story answers, so nothing real gets hidden. Hidden comments aren't deleted. You can still see them under Hidden comments at the bottom of each post.

b. Tags and mentions (stops scam giveaway tagging)

  1. Tap Tags and mentions.
  2. Who can tag you → Allow tags from people you follow.
  3. Turn on Manually approve tags.
  4. Who can @mention you → People you follow.

c. Messages and story replies (keep the quiz replies flowing)

  1. Tap Messages and story replies → Message controls.
  2. For Others on Instagram, choose Message requests. Don't choose "Don't receive requests", or the A/B story replies from new people disappear.
  3. Back one screen, Story replies → Everyone (the nightly quiz depends on this).

d. Comments (leave open, on purpose)

Leave Comments → Allow comments from on Everyone. Comments from strangers are how new real people find the page, and the pinned-comment answer plan needs them. Hidden Words does the filtering instead.

e. Limits (only for a spam attack)

Don't switch this on now. If one day brings a flood of spam comments or DMs: Limits → turn on Accounts that aren't following you and Recent followers → choose 1 week. It quietens everything, including real new fans, so switch it off when the wave stops.

f. Lock the account (bots also come from hacked pages)

  1. ☰ → Accounts Centre → Password and security → Two-factor authentication → pick the Instagram account → Authentication app (or text message if you prefer).
  2. Same screen: Where you're logged in. Log out any device you don't recognise. Metricool doesn't appear here, because it connects through Meta's official partner access.
  3. Same screen: Login alerts → on, sent to hello@moodius.co.uk.

3. Grow only in ways Instagram counts as real Standing rule

Instagram quietly limits the reach of accounts it thinks are faking growth. These rules keep the page on the right side of that, and I'll follow them in everything I schedule.

Never

  • Buy followers, likes, views or comments, at any price
  • Join engagement pods or "comment for comment" groups
  • Follow-then-unfollow, or follow lots of accounts to get follows back
  • Give your Instagram password to any growth app or "manager" (Metricool doesn't need it)
  • Use bot hashtags: #followforfollow, #f4f, #like4like, #instagood, #explorepage
  • "Tag 3 friends to win" giveaways
  • Auto-commenting tools (your comment agent only drafts, you send)

Do

  • 3-5 specific hashtags per post (#stoicism, #journaling, #emotionalhealth…), never 30
  • "Send this to the friend who…" instead of tag-baiting
  • By hand, leave a few real, full-sentence comments a day on similar pages
  • Follow only pages you'd actually read
  • Reply to every real comment in the first hour
  • Collaborate with real small pages (Collab posts) once you're ready

One question for you: the page now follows 70 accounts, up from 22 last week. If you followed those by hand because you like them, that's fine. If any app or service is doing it, tell me and we'll stop it. That pattern gets pages flagged.

4. When we run the first ad Later · needs your OK

No ad is running and none will start without your yes on the budget. When the first test (€35, about £30) happens, these settings keep the money on real people:

  • Goal: website sign-ups, not "more followers" or "profile visits". Follower-goal ads are where most click-farm and fake followers come from, because they're the cheapest thing to fake.
  • Placements: Instagram only (Feed, Reels, Stories). Untick Audience Network and Messenger. Audience Network is the main source of junk clicks.
  • Location: United Kingdom, "people living in" (not "recently in"), plus Ireland if you want. Your followers are 86% UK already.
  • Audience: ages 22-45, interests Stoicism, philosophy, journaling, self-help books. Turn off "Advantage+ audience" expansion for the first test so we can see who it reaches.
  • Check after 48 hours: if clicks are high but sign-ups are zero, pause it. That's the sign of bot clicks.
