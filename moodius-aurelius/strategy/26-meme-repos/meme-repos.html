Five repos that would make the memes better

I checked 18 free tools against the meme workflow you just approved (find, dedupe, draw, approve, measure). Anything already judged in earlier threads was skipped. Nothing here costs money, needs an Instagram login, or uses faces.

Short version: two are clear wins and I tested both here.

1. ImageHash stops the same meme going out twice, even cropped or re-saved.

2. Rough.js gives the chart memes a hand-drawn look that fits the brand.

The rest are optional. No tool can judge whether a joke is funny; your story polls already do that best.

Top five, ranked
1ImageHash🟢 Use
github.com/JohannesBuchner/imagehash

Catches a meme we already posted or logged, even if it was cropped, shrunk or saved as a JPEG. Today the queue only compares links and caption text.

Owner Johannes Buchner (individual)Last commit 12 Oct 2026Licence BSD-2Advisories none listedCost £0Size 4 small dependencies

Tested here: I cropped, halved and re-saved MB01 as a low-quality JPEG. ImageHash still matched it exactly (distance 0). Your 20 memes all come out at least 8 apart, so a cut-off of about 4 flags copies without blocking new memes in the same style.

2Rough.js🟢 Use
github.com/rough-stuff/rough

Draws lines, circles and charts that look sketched by hand, which matches the "hand-made texture over clean vector" rule. You approved the chart and observation memes and passed on the self-mocking ones, so this is where it pays off.

Owner Preet Shihn (individual)Last release Nov 2023 (finished, stable)Licence MITAdvisories none knownDependencies noneCost £0

The test is a draft to show the look, not a meme for your approval.

3Excalidraw🟢 For you, no install
github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw · excalidraw.com

A free sketch board in your browser. When a meme idea hits, scribble it in 30 seconds and send me a screenshot. I redraw it in house style.

Owner Excalidraw (company, open source)Last commit 23 Oct 2026Licence MITAdvisories none listed for the appCost £0, no account needed
4Typst🟠 Later, if needed
github.com/typst/typst

A layout engine with proper typesetting: even line breaks, kerning, hyphenation. It would replace our hand-built text wrapping. Only worth it if memes start needing longer or mixed text.

Owner Typst GmbHLast commit 26 Oct 2026Licence Apache-2.0Advisories none listedCost £0Effort a renderer rewrite
5ruptures🟠 Tracker, from mid-Dec
github.com/deepcharles/ruptures

For the growth tracker: finds the exact day growth changed pace (a bot wave, or a post that took off). It needs about 60 days of data, so not before mid-December.

Owner ENS Paris-Saclay researchersLast commit 26 May 2026Licence BSD-2Advisories none listedCost £0, runs on your Mac
