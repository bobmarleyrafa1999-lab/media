# 16. Lessons from the first 19 posts (22 Oct 2026)

Source: Metricool analytics for brand 7224060, every post published 6-21 Oct (13 reels, 5 single images, 1 carousel), pulled 22 Oct 2026. Per-post numbers: `clear-out-2026-10-22/ranking.csv`. Nothing published 16-20 Oct (the free-plan cap), so that week is missing.

**How much to trust this.** It's 19 posts and about 14 followers. Each finding below is labelled. *Measured* means it held across every post in the group. *Likely* means the pattern is clear but the groups overlap (for example every 10:00 reel was also a discipline or productivity reel), so the cause can't be pinned to one thing. Nothing here is a study; it's this account's own numbers.

## The short version

| Keep doing | Stop doing |
|---|---|
| Reels, every time | Single-quote image cards |
| Hooks about another person or a conflict | Memes |
| 12:00 and 18:00 slots | Anything at 10:00, and weekend evening posts at 19:00 |
| Emotion, hurt, anger, approval topics | Discipline, motivation and productivity pep talks |
| A two-branch "test" structure (if X, then...; if not, then...) | Numbered lists as the opening hook |
| Two reels a day at most | Three reels in one day |

## By format

- **Reels carry the account (measured).** Median reel reach at 12:00/18:00 on weekdays was 112. The five single images averaged 5. Every save and every share on the account came from a reel.
- **Quote cards are wasted effort (measured).** Four cards, reach 4, 4, 4 and 5. They reach almost nobody but the existing 14 followers. None are scheduled again; keep it that way.
- **Memes are wasted effort (measured, n=1).** Reach 9, and the one meme carried retired #youngmen framing.
- **Carousels are unproven.** One so far (21 Oct), reach 13 after a day. Eleven are queued 22 Oct-1 Nov as the X1 cover test. If the 22-25 Oct carousels all stay under 20 reach, X1 can't produce an answer and should be called early. The reel-first plan from 2 Nov (2 carousels a week) already fits this data.

## By hook type

- **Conflict and other-person hooks win (measured).** "Someone disrespected you today?", "Not every insult deserves a reply", "The fastest way to lose an argument? Get angry", "You can't make everyone like you", "It's the story you tell about it": reach 106-122, and 37-47% of viewers stayed past 3 seconds. That's the best hold on the account, every time.
- **Numbered-list hooks hold the fewest people (measured).** "3 Stoic rules", "5 things you can't control", "Three signs you're regulating": 25-29% past 3s, the three lowest holds of any reel. Reach can still be fine (119, 104), but people swipe sooner.
- **Self call-outs flop (likely).** "Motivation isn't coming" (20) and "Busy all day. Nothing done." (23). Both also went out at 10:00, so slot and hook overlap.
- **Authority and ritual hooks are a coin flip.** "Marcus Aurelius said this to himself every morning" had the top reach (131) but a weak 25% hold; "Marcus Aurelius did this every night" got 32.
- **What held people longest (measured, n=1):** "Someone disrespected you today?" was watched for 16.5s on average; every other reel was 2.7-5.7s. It's a question hook followed by a two-branch test ("if it's true... if it's false..."), so the viewer waits for the second branch. Worth using that shape again on purpose.

## Retention

- **People leave between second 3 and second 6 (measured).** Average watch was 4-5.7s on reels of about 19-22s, so roughly a quarter of the reel gets seen. The hook earns the first 3 seconds; the second line loses them. The second line must pay off the hook straight away, not set up context.
- **Saves and shares are tiny (measured).** 5 saves and 6 shares across 13 reels. The "Save this" and "Send this to..." caption lines aren't doing much yet; the share that matters has to be earned on screen, not asked for in the caption.

## By time slot (Madrid)

- **12:00 and 18:00 work (measured).** Ten reels in those slots: nine got reach 104-131. The one miss (13 Oct 12:04, reach 18) was the second of three reels that day.
- **10:00 is dead (likely).** Two reels at 10:00 got 20 and 23; four images at 10:00 got 4-9. The reel-first plan has no 10:00 slot. Keep it that way.
- **Three reels in a day cannibalise each other (likely).** On 13 Oct the first two reels got 20 and 18 and the third got 131. Two a day, as the reel-first plan does, looks like the ceiling at this size.
- **Weekend 19:00 underperformed (n=2).** Sat 10 Oct reel 32, Sun 11 Oct image 4. The plan already cuts the Saturday 12:00 reel if it stays under 60 reach twice.

## By topic

- **Works:** other people, anger, insults, approval, hurt and the story you tell about it, overthinking. All of it is "Stoicism for people who feel things".
- **Doesn't:** discipline, motivation, productivity, routines told as advice. These are the crowded "grindset" Stoic topics, and they're off the positioning anyway.
- **Off-brand and now archived:** the "good man... more being him" card and the #youngmen meme.

## Rules for the queue from today

1. Every reel opens on another person or a feeling, not a list and not a pep talk. List reels need a conflict line first ("Someone said this to you. Here are 3 ways to answer.").
2. The second line pays off the hook within 3 seconds.
3. Use the two-branch test structure in at least one reel a week.
4. Never two reels within 2 hours, never three in a day.
5. No single-quote images and no memes on the grid. Quotes go inside reels, carousels or stories.
6. Discipline topics only when reframed as a feeling ("You don't have a discipline problem, you have a self-trust problem").
7. No gendered framing on screen or in captions. The 27 Oct reel ("1 in 4 young men...") and the 30 Oct #masculinity hashtag were flagged to the account thread on 22 Oct.

## How many posts the profile should show

There is no magic total. What decides a follow is the first screen. On a phone a profile shows the first 3-4 rows of the 3-column grid, so **9-12 posts** is the window a visitor judges you by. Instagram's grid tiles are now tall (3:4) rectangles, so covers need their text in the middle of the frame.

At about 14 followers, 93% of the people reaching you are strangers who found a reel. Some tap your name, see those 9-12 tiles and decide in a few seconds. So the profile is a shop window, and:

- **Below 9 posts the page looks new or abandoned.** That's why the clear-out stops at 10. Archiving all eight old-look reels would leave 2 posts, which looks worse than the old style does.
- **Above 12, more posts don't hurt.** Hardly anyone scrolls past row 4, and old posts below it cost nothing. Big accounts have hundreds.
- **Consistency of the top 12 matters more than the count.** Same palette, same type, same cover layout, every tile readable at thumbnail size. Three pins sit in the first row, so they set the tone.
- **Rows don't need to be "complete".** Posting twice a day shifts the grid constantly, so planning in exact multiples of 3 isn't worth the effort.

So the target is simple: keep the top 12 tiles all in the Slate and Bone look with readable covers, never drop below 9 posts, and archive anything under about 25 reach once it's a week old, unless it's the only post on a topic people need to find.

On the 19 Nov review, rerun this: pull per-post metrics, archive anything older than 7 days with reach under 25 or off the positioning, and update this doc's rules.
