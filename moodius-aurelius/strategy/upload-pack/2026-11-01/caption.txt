The two horses pulling your mind.

Plato, Phaedrus 246b (Jowett): the soul as a charioteer with two horses, "one of them is noble and of noble breed, and the other is ignoble." The wild horse isn't the enemy. It's energy. You don't get rid of it, you train it.

Six steps: notice the pull, name what it wants, ask the other horse, shorten the reins, give the energy a job, review at night.
