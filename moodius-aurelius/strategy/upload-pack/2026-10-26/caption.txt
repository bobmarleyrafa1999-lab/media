You're not lazy. You're avoiding a feeling.

Most procrastination isn't a time problem. Psychologists link it to mood repair: you put the task off to escape how it makes you feel (doubt, boredom, fear of doing it badly). Marcus caught himself at it too. Meditations 2.4 (Long): "Remember how long you have been putting off these things."

Name the feeling. Shrink the start to two minutes. Bring the feeling with you.

Save this for the next thing you keep putting off.
