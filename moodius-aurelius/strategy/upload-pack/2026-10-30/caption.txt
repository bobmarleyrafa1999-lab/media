Who you let close becomes who you are.

The myth: you are who you are, whoever you're around. Aristotle disagreed. Nicomachean Ethics IX.12 (Ross): friends "take the mould of the characteristics they approve" from each other. And IX.4: "his friend is another self."

Pick friends you'd be glad to become.
