How to stop rehearsing disasters that never come.

Your mind runs the worst case on repeat because rehearsing feels like preparing. It isn't. It's living the bad day in advance, often more than once. Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 7.8 (Long): you will meet future things "having with you the same reason which now you use for present things."

Ask for the next action. If there isn't one, come back to the room.
