Why one comment ruins your whole day.

Ninety-nine kind ones, one sharp one, and you replay the sharp one. Psychologists call it negativity bias: the mind gives a threat more weight than praise. Epictetus, Enchiridion 20 (Higginson): "It is your own opinion which provokes you." The comment took ten seconds. The story you add to it takes the day.

Buy time. Don't reply today.
