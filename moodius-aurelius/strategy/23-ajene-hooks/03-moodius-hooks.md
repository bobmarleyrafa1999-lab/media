# Ajené Pinnock's hooks, rewritten for Moodius (25 Oct 2026)

Twelve hook formulas from his public videos, strongest fit for a faceless page first. Each has the evidence (his own post, platform, public numbers), why it works, Moodius rewrites, and the fact the rewrite rests on.

Fit: **strong** = use as is, **good** = use with care, **adapt** = shape changes for Moodius, **later** = not now.

Rules kept from the brand book: no faces, no invented stats, quotes, studies or results; quotes cite book and section and are checked against Standard Ebooks before posting; paraphrases say 'Moodius paraphrase'; 2 in 3 posts open from curiosity or appreciation; every post ends on a 1-3 word question; main share ask is 'send this to the friend who…'.

## A06 Don't do it yet (strong, check source)

**Shape:** Don't [do the thing you're about to do] yet… [reason in line 2].

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "Dont buy a new iPhone yet… iPhone 16 is 1 month away." (1.1M views, 78.4K likes (7%), 579x his median)
- YouTube Shorts: "Never Record Until You Know These Best Practices" (4.7K, 9x his Shorts median)

**Why it works:** It stops a decision the viewer is about to make. Waiting costs nothing, so they stay to hear why.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Don't send that text yet…
- Don't reply to that message tonight. Read this first.

**Fact check:** Line 2 can be Seneca's advice to wait out anger (On Anger). Check the exact book and section against Standard Ebooks before it goes in a post.

## A02 Labelled-role skit (strong, ready)

**Shape:** Two characters with name labels on screen, a short exchange, a funny last line.

**His evidence:**

- Instagram: "'Social Media Consultant' vs 'Online Coach/Consultant', ends 'Thank you' + a Follow button" (242K views, his biggest pinned reel)

**Why it works:** Viewers know both roles in one second, so there is no setup. The labels do the explaining.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Label left: 'You, 2am'. Label right: 'Epictetus, Nicopolis'. You: 'What if they're angry with me?' Epictetus: 'Is their anger yours to run?' You: '…going back to sleep.' (Moodius paraphrase, labelled.)

**Fact check:** Epictetus taught at Nicopolis after Domitian expelled philosophers (library EP010, verified). The line is a Moodius paraphrase of 'up to us / not up to us' and must say so on screen.

## A03 Headline + twist subline (strong, ready)

**Shape:** Big claim on line 1, a small line under it that turns it into a joke on yourself.

**His evidence:**

- Instagram: "I fired my video editor and hired Claude (By the way, that editor was me 😂)" (pinned grid, views not shown)
- TikTok: "£7 a video is crazy. I guess we all start somewhere lol." (11.1K, 5.8x)

**Why it works:** The subline rewards people who read it, and the self-own makes the account feel human without a face.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- I stopped arguing with the person who ruins my mornings. (It was me. It was always me.)
- An emperor wrote twelve books of advice. (He was the only reader he wanted.)

**Fact check:** Meditations has 12 books and was written to himself; its Greek title is usually given as 'To Himself'. Standard history, check wording before use.

## A10 Tip for a named problem (strong, check source)

**Shape:** Useful [kind of] tip if you keep [specific annoying thing].

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "Useful Mac tip if you keep running out of storage" (117.6K, 62x)
- YouTube Shorts: "Filming Tips for Talking Head Videos" (23K, 44x)

**Why it works:** The viewer self-selects in four words. If they keep doing the thing, the video is for them.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Useful trick if you keep checking whether they replied.

**Fact check:** Body: sort the thought into 'mine to change' and 'not mine', Epictetus's opening rule (Enchiridion 1). Check translation wording before use.

## A12 Question about the obvious odd thing (strong, ready)

**Shape:** Why [is this strange thing everyone has seen] like that?

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "Why are iPads bezels so big?????" (125.6K, 66x)
- YouTube Shorts: "The Best Wireless Microphone?" (231K, 444x his Shorts median)

**Why it works:** Everyone has noticed it and nobody has asked. Curiosity, not pain, does the work, which fits the 2-in-3 appreciation rule.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Why did the most powerful man alive keep a diary nobody was meant to read?
- Why did an enslaved teacher end up on an emperor's bookshelf?

**Fact check:** Epictetus was enslaved in Rome (EP003) and Marcus thanks Rusticus for lending him Epictetus's discourses, Meditations 1.7 (EP026). Both verified in the library.

## A07 Big name, against the crowd (good, check source)

**Shape:** [Famous name] created his own problem… [your calmer verdict].

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "MKBHD created his own problem… I think people are just overreacting." (1M views, 30.9K likes, 527x)
- YouTube long: "YouTube Changed Forever & What's Working In Content Right Now..." (13K, 4.3x)
- TikTok (miss): "They say never upload short form in 4K… I say different." (1.4K, 0.7x: no name, so it fell flat)

**Why it works:** A name people already know plus a verdict they can disagree with. The miss shows the name is what carries it.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Marcus Aurelius created his own problem… and it was his son.
- The wisest emperor in Rome made his worst call at home.

**Fact check:** History pillar: Marcus made Commodus co-emperor, and Commodus's reign is remembered badly. Check dates against a public-domain history (for example Gibbon) before use.

## A08 Low expectations, then a score (strong, ready)

**Shape:** I didn't think I'd like [it]. [Score]/10.

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "I didn't think I was going to like it here tbf. 9/10." (300.4K views, 8,316 shares: the most shared of his 120 TikToks)

**Why it works:** An honest-sounding reversal plus a number people want to argue with. It drew shares, which is the metric that tracks reach for Moodius.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Rating Stoic habits I expected to hate. Getting up early: 4/10. Writing down three lines at night: 9/10.
- I didn't think a book this old would get me. 9/10.

**Fact check:** Scores are the page's opinion, not data. Fine as voice. Make it a 'you pick the next one' Friday story.

## A11 X >> Y verdict (good, ready)

**Shape:** [Thing] >> [thing]. or Hands down the best [n] [things].

**His evidence:**

- TikTok: "Apple Watch >> Whoop." (88.5K, 47x)
- TikTok: "Hands down the best 3 premiere pro plugins" (129.9K, 68x)

**Why it works:** A verdict in two words. It matches the Moodius hook bank, where X vs Y openers ran 3.4x.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Writing it down >> talking yourself in circles.
- Bear and forbear >> 'just stay positive'.

**Fact check:** 'Bear and forbear' is Epictetus's two-word rule as reported by Gellius, Attic Nights 17.19 (EP025, verified).

**His evidence:**

**Fact check:** Moodius has no client results and must never invent stats or ROI, so the 'receipt' is always from the historical record (EP003 + EP026, verified).

## A13 Identity order, big outcome (adapt, check source)

**Shape:** You need to treat yourself like [role]. / How to build [trait] so strong it's impossible to [fail].

**His evidence:**

**Why it works:** His self-improvement titles beat his business titles by a wide margin.

**Moodius rewrites:**

**Fact check:** Keep the athlete image, drop the hype words ('delusional', 'kill'): pep talks are on the Moodius wasted-effort list. Enchiridion 29 is the Olympics passage; check before use.

## A04 Comment a keyword (later, needs your OK later)

**Shape:** Comment "[word]" and I'll send you [thing]. Reverse version: Don't comment [word] here, go to the link.

**His evidence:**

- Instagram: "Comment "templates" for the AI editing pack" (pinned grid, views not shown)
- TikTok: "Don't comment TEMPLATES here, go to the 🔗 in my bio!" (939 views but 18 comments, 4.5x his median comment count)

**Why it works:** It turns a viewer into a lead. The reverse version still pulled comments on a weak-view post.

**Moodius rewrites:**

- Later, when there is a real thing to send: Comment LETTER and I'll send you Seneca's letter on anger, one page.

**Fact check:** Not now. Moodius has nothing to send yet, Metricool can't read comments, and the comment agent only drafts, so every keyword means you replying by hand. Keep 'send this to the friend who…' as the main ask.
