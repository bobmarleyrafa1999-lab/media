## Editing style seen on the Instagram grid

- Hook in a white rounded text box in the top third, with one word picked out in an orange box ("and hired **Claude**", "**like my**").
- Two lines: a big headline and a smaller subline that twists it ("By the way, that editor was me 😂").
- Split screen: two clips stacked top and bottom.
- Name labels with arrows on people in skits, and mock interface pieces (a Follow button, a prompt box).
- Screen recordings next to talking head for demos.

Faceless translation for the reels engine: Bone text box on Slate with the key word in an Ember box; labels and chat bubbles instead of faces; generic interface shapes only, never a real app's logo.
