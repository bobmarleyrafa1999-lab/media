Ajené Pinnock's hooks, rebuilt for a faceless page

I collected 267 of his public hooks across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube and sorted the ones that beat his own average into 12 formulas. Each one below has a Moodius rewrite that needs no face and no made-up numbers.

Try these three first

  • A06Don't do it yet. "Don't send that text yet…" His version reached 1.1M with a 7% like rate. It is a conflict opener, which already works for Moodius.
  • A02Labelled-role skit. "You, 2am" vs "Epictetus". His biggest visible reel (242K) is a skit with name labels, and labels work without faces.
  • A12Question about the obvious odd thing. "Why did the most powerful man alive keep a diary nobody was meant to read?" Curiosity, not hurt, so it fits the 2-in-3 positive rule.

The twelve formulas

"x" means times his own median on that platform (TikTok 1,900 views, YouTube long-form 3,050, Shorts 520). Most formulas rest on one or two of his posts, so treat them as leads to test.

A06

Don't do it yet

Use as is

Don't [do the thing you're about to do] yet… [reason in line 2].

His version

  • TikTok Dont buy a new iPhone yet… iPhone 16 is 1 month away. 1.1M views, 78.4K likes (7%), 579x his median
  • YouTube Shorts Never Record Until You Know These Best Practices 4.7K, 9x his Shorts median

It stops a decision the viewer is about to make. Waiting costs nothing, so they stay to hear why.

Moodius rewrite

Don't send that text yet…
Don't reply to that message tonight. Read this first.

Check source. Line 2 can be Seneca's advice to wait out anger (On Anger). Check the exact book and section against Standard Ebooks before it goes in a post.

A02

Labelled-role skit

Use as is

Two characters with name labels on screen, a short exchange, a funny last line.

His version

  • Instagram 'Social Media Consultant' vs 'Online Coach/Consultant', ends 'Thank you' + a Follow button 242K views, his biggest pinned reel

Viewers know both roles in one second, so there is no setup. The labels do the explaining.

Moodius rewrite

Label left: 'You, 2am'. Label right: 'Epictetus, Nicopolis'. You: 'What if they're angry with me?' Epictetus: 'Is their anger yours to run?' You: '…going back to sleep.' (Moodius paraphrase, labelled.)

Ready. Epictetus taught at Nicopolis after Domitian expelled philosophers (library EP010, verified). The line is a Moodius paraphrase of 'up to us / not up to us' and must say so on screen.

A03

Headline + twist subline

Use as is

Big claim on line 1, a small line under it that turns it into a joke on yourself.

His version

  • Instagram I fired my video editor and hired Claude (By the way, that editor was me 😂) pinned grid, views not shown
  • TikTok £7 a video is crazy. I guess we all start somewhere lol. 11.1K, 5.8x

The subline rewards people who read it, and the self-own makes the account feel human without a face.

Moodius rewrite

I stopped arguing with the person who ruins my mornings. (It was me. It was always me.)
An emperor wrote twelve books of advice. (He was the only reader he wanted.)

Ready. Meditations has 12 books and was written to himself; its Greek title is usually given as 'To Himself'. Standard history, check wording before use.

A10

Tip for a named problem

Use as is

Useful [kind of] tip if you keep [specific annoying thing].

His version

  • TikTok Useful Mac tip if you keep running out of storage 117.6K, 62x
  • YouTube Shorts Filming Tips for Talking Head Videos 23K, 44x

The viewer self-selects in four words. If they keep doing the thing, the video is for them.

Moodius rewrite

Useful Stoic trick if you keep replaying that conversation.
Useful trick if you keep checking whether they replied.

Check source. Body: sort the thought into 'mine to change' and 'not mine', Epictetus's opening rule (Enchiridion 1). Check translation wording before use.

A12

Question about the obvious odd thing

Use as is

Why [is this strange thing everyone has seen] like that?

His version

  • TikTok Why are iPads bezels so big????? 125.6K, 66x
  • YouTube Shorts The Best Wireless Microphone? 231K, 444x his Shorts median

Everyone has noticed it and nobody has asked. Curiosity, not pain, does the work, which fits the 2-in-3 appreciation rule.

Moodius rewrite

Why did the most powerful man alive keep a diary nobody was meant to read?
Why did an enslaved teacher end up on an emperor's bookshelf?

Ready. Epictetus was enslaved in Rome (EP003) and Marcus thanks Rusticus for lending him Epictetus's discourses, Meditations 1.7 (EP026). Both verified in the library.

A07

Big name, against the crowd

Use with care

[Famous name] created his own problem… [your calmer verdict].

His version

  • TikTok MKBHD created his own problem… I think people are just overreacting. 1M views, 30.9K likes, 527x
  • YouTube long YouTube Changed Forever & What's Working In Content Right Now... 13K, 4.3x
  • TikTok (miss) They say never upload short form in 4K… I say different. 1.4K, 0.7x: no name, so it fell flat

A name people already know plus a verdict they can disagree with. The miss shows the name is what carries it.

Moodius rewrite

Marcus Aurelius created his own problem… and it was his son.
The wisest emperor in Rome made his worst call at home.

Check source. History pillar: Marcus made Commodus co-emperor, and Commodus's reign is remembered badly. Check dates against a public-domain history (for example Gibbon) before use.

A08

Low expectations, then a score

Use as is

I didn't think I'd like [it]. [Score]/10.

His version

  • TikTok I didn't think I was going to like it here tbf. 9/10. 300.4K views, 8,316 shares: the most shared of his 120 TikToks

An honest-sounding reversal plus a number people want to argue with. It drew shares, which is the metric that tracks reach for Moodius.

Moodius rewrite

Rating Stoic habits I expected to hate. Getting up early: 4/10. Writing down three lines at night: 9/10.
I didn't think a book this old would get me. 9/10.

Ready. Scores are the page's opinion, not data. Fine as voice. Make it a 'you pick the next one' Friday story.

A11

X >> Y verdict

Use with care

[Thing] >> [thing]. or Hands down the best [n] [things].

His version

  • TikTok Apple Watch >> Whoop. 88.5K, 47x
  • TikTok Hands down the best 3 premiere pro plugins 129.9K, 68x

A verdict in two words. It matches the Moodius hook bank, where X vs Y openers ran 3.4x.

Moodius rewrite

Writing it down >> talking yourself in circles.
Bear and forbear >> 'just stay positive'.

Ready. 'Bear and forbear' is Epictetus's two-word rule as reported by Gellius, Attic Nights 17.19 (EP025, verified).

A01

Receipts first

Changed for Moodius

Show the result and who got it before you explain anything.

His version

  • Instagram Coach Makes $3000 MRR + Closed $8K Client 74.5K, pinned
  • YouTube long How I Edit My Videos Using Claude Code (Fully Automated) 42K, 14x his long-form median
  • TikTok How much YouTube paid me for 100k views… 14.3K, 7.5x

Proof before promise. His client case on the agency page backs the numbers.

Moodius rewrite

Born enslaved. Ended up on an emperor's bookshelf.

Ready. Moodius has no client results and must never invent stats or ROI, so the 'receipt' is always from the historical record (EP003 + EP026, verified).

A05

Reintroduce yourself (pinned)

Use as is

A pinned reel that says who the page is for and what they get, made for new visitors.

His version

  • Instagram 'to reintroduce' (him pointing at the camera) 15.6K, pinned

The top 9-12 tiles decide whether a visitor follows. A pinned intro answers 'what is this page' in one tap.

Moodius rewrite

Time to reintroduce this page. No face. No name. Just old notes that keep working on new problems.

Ready. Faceless by design: the anonymity becomes the hook. Fits the 90-day plan's 'pin 3' action.

A13

Identity order, big outcome

Changed for Moodius

You need to treat yourself like [role]. / How to build [trait] so strong it's impossible to [fail].

His version

  • YouTube long You Need To Treat Yourself Like An Athlete 116K, 38x
  • YouTube long You need to kill the old version of yourself & become delusional 52K, 17x
  • YouTube long (miss) How to Turn Your Viewers Into Thirsty Buyers 341, 0.1x

His self-improvement titles beat his business titles by a wide margin.

Moodius rewrite

Epictetus said to train like you're going to the Olympics. Here is the training plan. (Carousel title.)

Check source. Keep the athlete image, drop the hype words ('delusional', 'kill'): pep talks are on the Moodius wasted-effort list. Enchiridion 29 is the Olympics passage; check before use.

A04

Comment a keyword

Not now

Comment "[word]" and I'll send you [thing]. Reverse version: Don't comment [word] here, go to the link.

His version

  • Instagram Comment "templates" for the AI editing pack pinned grid, views not shown
  • TikTok Don't comment TEMPLATES here, go to the 🔗 in my bio! 939 views but 18 comments, 4.5x his median comment count

It turns a viewer into a lead. The reverse version still pulled comments on a weak-view post.

Moodius rewrite

Later, when there is a real thing to send: Comment LETTER and I'll send you Seneca's letter on anger, one page.

Needs your OK later. Not now. Moodius has nothing to send yet, Metricool can't read comments, and the comment agent only drafts, so every keyword means you replying by hand. Keep 'send this to the friend who…' as the main ask.

His editing style, translated

He puts the hook in a white text box in the top third with one word in an orange box, adds a small twist line under the headline, stacks two clips in a split screen, and labels people with arrows. For Moodius the same grammar works with a Bone text box on Slate, the key word in Ember, labels and chat bubbles instead of faces, and plain interface shapes rather than any real app's logo.

What I could not reach

Instagram is blocked from here, so his Instagram hooks are only the six tiles in your screenshot. YouTube transcripts need a sign-in, so I have no spoken opening lines. To get them, the transcript tool would have to run on your Mac, which takes about 15 minutes of your time. That is optional and can wait.
