A short play in two acts. Act one: someone texts you "ok". Act two: you lie awake writing their whole inner life for them.

Epictetus taught this almost 1,900 years ago: the message didn't keep you up, the story you told yourself about it did. The dialogue is ours, the idea is his.

Send this to the friend who reads "ok" as a threat. 🫠

Angry or busy?

— Moodius
