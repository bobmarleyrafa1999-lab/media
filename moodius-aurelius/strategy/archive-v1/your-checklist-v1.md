They're attached to the thread message: cover-start, cover-purpose, cover-suffering, cover-amorfati, cover-virtue, cover-beauty. Open each one and save it to your camera roll.

## 2. Update your profile (3 min)
Instagram > Profile > **Edit profile**:
- **Name:** `Moodius Aurelius | Stoic Wisdom`. The name field is searchable, so "Stoic Wisdom" helps people find you.
- **Bio:** paste this exactly:
```
Ancient wisdom for young men.
Purpose · Suffering · Amor fati · Virtue
3 posts a day. Free guide ↓
```
- **Category:** Edit profile > Category > "Writer" or "Education". This only works on a Professional account. If you don't see it, switch first: Settings > Account type and tools > Switch to professional account > Creator.
- **Link:** leave it for now. You'll add the free guide link in step 5.

## 3. Create the 6 highlights (after tonight's first story)
The first story goes live **tonight at 21:00** (Madrid). Then:
1. Open your profile, tap your profile picture to view the story.
2. Tap **Highlight** (bottom right) > **New** > name it **Start** > Add.
3. On your profile, press and hold the new highlight > **Edit highlight** > **Edit cover** > tap the photo icon > pick **cover-start** > Done.

**Order:** Instagram puts the highlight you most recently added a story to first. That's fine, it doesn't need to be in a set order.
