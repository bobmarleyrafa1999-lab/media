## F1 Confrontational "you" reel
- **Structure (6 cards):** Hook (3.0s, big) → Who said it (2.4s) → Quote (3.6–4.2s, big) → Book + section (1.8s) → One-line application (3.0s) → CTA (2.4s).
- **Hook formulas:** "You keep ___ people who ___." · "Nobody is coming to ___." · "You don't need another ___." · "They got away with it. You didn't." Name a relationship or an insult. Those held best.
- **Never:** numbers in the hook ("3 rules…"), soft openers ("A reminder that…").
- **CTA rotation:** send to a brother / save for later / follow for one rule a day.
- **Platform versions:** IG Reel → YouTube Short (same file, title = hook) → TikTok (add native text-to-speech or trending sound in-app) → Facebook Reel (cross-post).

## F2 Myth-bust reel
- **Structure:** "___? The Stoics disagree." → source → short quote → section → reframe → save CTA.
- **Myths to bust:** Stoics don't feel. Stoicism is just not caring. Discipline means hating yourself. Being alone means something's wrong with you. Real men don't ask for help.
- **Variant:** reply-to-comment reel once real comments arrive (TikTok's reply format).

## F3 "5 ancient lines" carousel
- **Slides:** 1 cover (promise + "Swipe →") · 2–6 one quote each: quote, source, one-line takeaway · 7 "Your turn" question + save/send CTA.
- **Rules:** mix authors; each line a different situation; takeaway ≤ 14 words; every source has a section number.
- **Caption:** the one line Moodius would keep, why, then the question.

## F4 Ancient practice ↔ modern evidence carousel
- **Slides:** cover ("3 Stoic habits psychology tested 2,000 years later") · 3 slides: ancient quote, modern concept name, one study (author, year) · honest-note slide ("science didn't prove Stoicism") · CTA.
- **Hard rules:** only named, real studies or concepts; "linked to", never "proves"; no therapy advice.
- **Bank of safe pairings:** reappraisal (Gross 1998; Ellis credited Epictetus) · expressive writing (Pennebaker & Beall 1986) · if-then plans (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) · affect labelling (Lieberman et al. 2007) · negative visualisation ↔ defensive pessimism (Norem) · delayed gratification (Mischel's marshmallow studies, with the 2018 replication caveat by Watts et al.).

## F5 Modern-life text meme
- **Layout:** cream background, two panels: "Me: *modern behaviour*" / "*the overreaction*", then "[Philosopher], [N] years ago:" / the line. Source under it.
- **Topics:** story views, read receipts, gym excuses, online arguments, 2am overthinking, texting an ex, comparing on LinkedIn.
- **Tone:** laughing *with* the reader. One meme a week maximum until data says more.

## F6 Problem → one practice reel
- **Structure:** "You ___ (problem)." → "Do this tonight:" → the practice in ≤ 3 steps → source → "Save this."
- **Practice bank:** evening review (Seneca, On Anger 3.36), premeditation of evils (Seneca Letters 91), view from above (Marcus 9.30), the pause before replying, a 10-minute walk alone.

## F7 Micro-story reel
- **Structure:** "In [year], [person] [lost / was exiled / was enslaved]." → 3 beats of story → what he wrote → the lesson for you → follow CTA. 25–35s, 7–9 cards.
- **Stories:** Seneca's exile to Corsica, Epictetus' slavery and limp, Marcus during the Antonine plague, Socrates' trial, Frankl in the camps, Cato's last night.
- **Accuracy:** label legends as legends ("According to Origen…").
- **Upgrade path:** human voice-over (Surafel's recordings) or a licensed TTS voice.

## F8 Framework diagram carousel
- **Slides:** cover question · diagram slide (two columns, circles, or a 2×2) · 3–5 slides applying it to young-men situations · CTA.
- **Frameworks:** dichotomy of control (two circles), two handles (Ench. 43), Plato's charioteer (Phaedrus 246a–254e), Aristotle's mean (cowardice / courage / recklessness), Marcus' view from above.

## F9 Quiet Truth
- **Image:** one sentence, big serif, no quote marks, pillar label "Quiet Truths".
- **Caption:** 150–250 words. Hook line → a short Moodius story or observation → the ancient line with source → the practice → one question.
- **Used to test:** long vs short captions (E3).

## F10 Daily Stoic / Feel Log story pair
- **09:00 Daily Stoic:** quote + source + one action for today. Highlight label = theme.
- **21:00 Feel Log:** one journaling prompt ("What did you avoid today, and why?"). Add a question sticker by hand when you can. The API can't add stickers.
- **Sunday 21:00:** a poll story ("Which line did you need this week? A / B"), added by hand.
