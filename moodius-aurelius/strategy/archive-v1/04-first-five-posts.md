**Caption**
```
Real men don't cry? The Stoics disagree.

Save this for the day you need it.

#stoicism #seneca #grief #mentalhealth #emotionalintelligence #youngmen #stoic #mensmentalhealth
```

## Post 4 · F4 Ancient ↔ modern evidence carousel · Psychology · Thu 29 Oct 15:00
**Files:** `carousel-22.jpg` … `carousel-27.jpg` (6 slides)
1. 3 Stoic habits psychology tested 2,000 years later.
2. Name the judgment. Marcus, Meditations 8.47 → cognitive reappraisal; Albert Ellis, a founder of CBT, credited Epictetus.
3. Review the day. Seneca, On Anger 3.36: "I conceal nothing from myself, I omit nothing." → expressive writing (Pennebaker & Beall, 1986).
4. Rehearse the moment. Epictetus, Enchiridion 4 → if-then plans; review of 94 studies (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006).
5. Honest note: science didn't "prove Stoicism". It found some of the same tools work.
6. Which one tonight? Try one for 7 days.

**Caption**
```
The Stoics didn't have labs. They had notebooks.

My pick for this week: the evening review. 5 minutes, honest, no guilt.

Save this and try one for 7 days. Then tell me what changed.

#stoicism #psychology #cbt #journaling #selfimprovement #habits #mentalhealth #stoic
```
