# Your checklist for @moodiusaurelius (Phase 1, from 21 Oct 2026)

**Claude does automatically:** writes, renders, checks and schedules every carousel, reel and story; writes captions, first-comment questions and alt text; pulls metrics, runs the experiments, and sends a Friday report with next week's drafts (sticker stories, broadcast posts, email letter). Everything below is what Instagram or the other tools don't let Claude do, so it's yours.

## A. One time (about 45 minutes in total)

**1. Profile (3 min).** Instagram > Profile > Edit profile.
- Name: `Moodius Aurelius | Stoicism for people who feel things`
- Bio:
```
Regulate, decide and act when emotions are loud.
Daily carousel + reel · Free guide ↓
```

**2. Highlights (15 min).** Save the 7 covers in `strategy/highlight-covers/` to your phone. Rebuild the highlights in this order, with these one-word names: **Manifesto · Daily · Tools · Discipline · Truths · Journal · Identity**.
- Rename old ones: press and hold > Edit highlight > change the name, then Edit cover > pick the new cover. Start → Manifesto, Purpose → Identity, Virtue → Discipline, Suffering → Truths (move Amor fati stories into it), Beauty → Journal.
- Create Daily and Tools when their first story goes live (Daily: Wed 21 Oct 09:00).
- Order: Instagram shows the highlight you added to most recently first. To set the order, add one story to each in reverse order: Identity, Journal, Truths, Discipline, Tools, Daily, Manifesto last.

**3. Free guide and email list (15 min).**
- Create a free **Kit** account (kit.com). Make a landing page with Claude's copy (in `strategy/11-community.md`, section 5) and attach `lead-magnet/7-stoic-tools-for-loud-emotions.pdf` as the incentive.
- Paste the welcome email from the same section.
- Put the landing-page link in your Instagram bio (Edit profile > Links).
- Tell Claude the link, so the Manifesto story and captions can point to it.

**4. Competitors in Metricool (2 min, computer).** Analytics > Instagram > Competitors > Add: `stoicism.daily`, `stoicismideas`, `theeverydaystoic`, `dailystoic`, `pathsofstoicism`.

**5. Metricool clean-up (5 min, only if Claude asks).** If the Metricool connector can't delete the 11 old Week 3 posts being replaced, Claude will list them and you delete them in Planning.

**6. Later, at about 100 followers:** create the broadcast channel "The Journal" (Inbox > pencil icon > Create broadcast channel), then post the "Join The Journal" story with the channel link sticker.

**Optional:** record 5–10 quotes in your own voice (quiet room, slowly, one file each) and upload them here, for the narrated reels (F6).

## B. Every day (about 15–20 minutes)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| 12:00–13:00 and 18:00–19:00 | Reply to every comment on the new post using the reply bank (`strategy/11-community.md`). Pin the best one. |
| Evening | Answer story replies and DMs (the 21:00 Journal story asks for replies). |
| Once a day | Add the day's two stories to their highlights. The highlight name is printed at the top of each story. Missed one? Profile > ☰ > Archive. |
| Tue and Sat | Post the sticker story Claude drafted (poll on Tue, question box on Sat). 2 minutes. |

If someone writes about self-harm or suicide, don't counsel them in comments. DM them the helpline line in section 1 of `11-community.md`.
