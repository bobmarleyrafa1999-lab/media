# Moodius Aurelius reels: research and scripts (4 Oct 2026)

## What the research says
- Instagram stopped recommending accounts that mostly repost other creators' reels (from 30 Apr 2026). Adding a border, watermark, subtitles or credit doesn't make a repost count as original. Source: creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026
- Top ranking signals on Reels are watch time and sends (DM shares), then likes per reach.
- Length: 45-60s reels had the highest median views in a 6M-reel study (wavevision.io). Stoic niche guides suggest 15-25s text or voiceover reels (flowshorts.app). These first three are 19-24s. Try a 45-60s voiceover reel once you have a voice.
- What works in the niche: a hook in the first 3 seconds, one idea per reel, a marble/classical look, and a closing line that asks people to save or send.

## Identity on every reel
"M · A" monogram with a gold rule at the top, @moodiusaurelius at the bottom, the same serif type, and a dark stone background.

## Reel 1: Dichotomy of control (23s)
You're stressed about things you can't control. / Marcus Aurelius ruled an empire. / Plague. War. Betrayal. / So he wrote himself one rule: / Some things are up to you. Most aren't. / Your judgments. Your actions. Your effort. / Everything else? Let it go. / Follow for one Stoic rule a day.
Caption: Write down one thing you're carrying today that isn't yours to carry. #stoicism #marcusaurelius #dichotomyofcontrol #stoic #mindset #anxiety

## Reel 2: Evening review (21s)
Marcus Aurelius did this every night. It takes 5 minutes. / Before bed, ask yourself: / 1. What did I do well today? / 2. Where did I fall short? / 3. What will I do differently tomorrow? / No guilt. Just honesty. / Save this for tonight.
Caption: Try it tonight and tell me which question was hardest. #stoicism #journaling #marcusaurelius #selfimprovement #nightroutine

## Reel 3: Handling disrespect (19s)
Someone disrespected you today? / The Stoics had a simple test. / Is what they said true? Then why be angry at the truth? / Is it false? Then it's their mistake, not yours. / Either way, your peace stays yours. / Send this to someone who needs it.
Caption: Based on Epictetus. Who needs to hear this? #stoicism #epictetus #stoic #mentalstrength #mindset
