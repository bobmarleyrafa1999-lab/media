# Moodius Aurelius publication: rules for Claude

This folder is the editorial system for the Moodius Aurelius Substack (strategy in `01-editorial-strategy.md`).

## Non-negotiable
- **Never publish, schedule or send anything externally** (Substack, Instagram, Pinterest, Threads, email) unless the asset's row in `queue/approval-queue.csv` has `approved_by` filled by Surafel. Drafts, exports and queues only.
- **Never invent** studies, statistics, quotes, citations, dates or personal anecdotes. If you can't open a source, say "canonical record, not re-opened" in `data/sources.csv` and in the essay's editor's notes.
- **Label every claim**: `[Study]`, `[Meta-analysis]`, `[Review]`, `[Survey]`, `[Theory]`, `[Philosophy]`, `[Interpretive framework]`, `[Idea: author]`, `[Moodius …]`.
- **Jung / shadow work** is an interpretive framework, never a validated treatment.
- **Quotes** only from public-domain translations with book and section (Long: Marcus, Epictetus; Gummere: Seneca's letters; Stewart: On Anger; Ross: Aristotle; Trotter: Pascal). Modern authors: paraphrase, "Idea: …".
- **Anonymous brand.** Byline "Moodius". No real names, faces, voices or locations.
- **Design:** Slate #1B1F24, Graphite #2A2F36, Charcoal #3A4049, Bone #F3EFE7, Ash #B8B3A9, Ember #C9742E (once); Playfair + Inter; no statues, marble, gold or gradients.

## Files
- `data/editorial.csv` (backlog) and `data/sources.csv` (research library) are the sources of truth. `data/editorial.db`, `03-backlog.md` and `vault/10-sources`, `vault/20-ideas` are generated by `scripts/build_db.py`.
- Essays: `essays/NN-slug.md` with `<!-- publish:start -->` / `<!-- publish:end -->` around publishable text.
- Packages: `packages/NN.json` → `scripts/render_packages.py` → `packages/NN-slug.md`, `packages/reels-batch.json`, `queue/approval-queue.csv`.
- Checks: `scripts/qc.py` (essays) and reels-engine `engine.py check` (reels) must pass before anything enters the queue.

## Commands
`/research-topic`, `/verify-sources`, `/draft-essay`, `/repurpose`, `/export-substack`, `/weekly-plan`, `/metrics-review`, `/add-idea` (in `.claude/commands/`).
