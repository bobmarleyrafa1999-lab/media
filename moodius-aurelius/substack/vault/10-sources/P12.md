# Carl Jung (1951): The shadow

**Finding:** The shadow is the disowned part of the personality; what we refuse to see in ourselves we often project onto others.

**Caveat:** Not empirical psychology; avoid 'shadow work' therapy claims.

**Angle:** The trait that irritates you most in other men is worth a second look.
