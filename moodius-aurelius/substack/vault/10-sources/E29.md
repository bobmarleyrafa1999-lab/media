# Stroebe & Schut (1999): Dual process model

**Finding:** Healthy grieving oscillates between facing the loss and attending to life (restoration), including taking breaks from grief.

**Caveat:** Model, not a timetable.

**Angle:** A normal afternoon isn't betraying them. Grief moves in and out.
