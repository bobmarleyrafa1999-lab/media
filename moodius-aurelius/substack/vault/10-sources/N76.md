# Davidai & Gilovich (2018): Ideal-self regrets endure

**Finding:** 72% of regrets concerned the ideal self vs 28% the ought self; 76% named an ideal-self regret as their biggest.

**Caveat:** Self-report; mostly US samples.

**Angle:** Take away the audience, find what you'd regret.
