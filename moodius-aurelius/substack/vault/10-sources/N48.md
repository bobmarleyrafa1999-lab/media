# Roisman, Padrón, Sroufe & Egeland (2002): Earned security

**Finding:** Prospectively earned-secure adults (insecure infants, secure at 19) had adult romantic relationships comparable to continuous-secures; retrospective earned-security did not track harsh early care.

**Caveat:** Small subgroups; definitions contested.

**Angle:** The destination is real even if the route isn't mapped.
