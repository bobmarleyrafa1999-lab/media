# World Health Organization (2019): Burn-out as an occupational phenomenon

**Finding:** Burn-out results from chronic workplace stress not successfully managed: exhaustion, mental distance/cynicism, reduced professional efficacy; occupational only, not a medical condition.

**Caveat:** Applies to work contexts only.

**Angle:** Not quite burnout, but its cousin.
