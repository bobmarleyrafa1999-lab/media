# Niemiec, Ryan & Deci (2009): Attaining extrinsic vs intrinsic aspirations

**Finding:** Progress on intrinsic aspirations predicted more wellbeing; progress on extrinsic (wealth, fame, image) did not and went with more ill-being.

**Caveat:** Small, well-educated US sample.

**Angle:** Reaching it isn't the same as being fed by it.
