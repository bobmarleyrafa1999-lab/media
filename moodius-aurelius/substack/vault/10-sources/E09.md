# Pennebaker & Beall; Frattaroli (meta-analysis) (1986; 2006): Expressive writing

**Finding:** Writing about difficult experiences for a few short sessions was linked to later health and wellbeing benefits; the meta-analysis finds a small average effect.

**Caveat:** Small effects; not a substitute for support.

**Angle:** Four nights, fifteen minutes, the thing you haven't said. That's the whole method.
