# Brennan, Clark & Shaver (1998): Attachment anxiety and avoidance as two dimensions

**Finding:** Adult attachment is better described by two dimensions, anxiety (fear of rejection) and avoidance (discomfort with closeness), than by types.

**Caveat:** Measure paper; don't turn it into a quiz-style diagnosis.

**Angle:** You're not 'an avoidant'. You have a dial that turns up when closeness feels risky.
