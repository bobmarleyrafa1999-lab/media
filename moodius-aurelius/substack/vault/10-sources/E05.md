# Gross & John (2003): Suppression vs reappraisal

**Finding:** Habitual suppression was associated with less positive and more negative emotion, poorer wellbeing and weaker social closeness; habitual reappraisal with the opposite.

**Caveat:** Correlational; suppression isn't always harmful in the moment.

**Angle:** Holding it in doesn't make it smaller. It makes you harder to reach.
