# Bonanno (2004): Resilience after loss

**Finding:** Resilience is a common response to loss; many bereaved people show relatively stable functioning, and it is not a sign of absent or delayed grief.

**Caveat:** Trajectory studies; not prescriptive.

**Angle:** Being okay is not proof you didn't love them.
