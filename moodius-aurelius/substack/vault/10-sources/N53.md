# Schkade & Kahneman (1998): Does living in California make people happy?

**Finding:** Overall life satisfaction was the same in the Midwest and Southern California though both groups predicted Californians would be happier; visible differences are overweighted.

**Caveat:** Students only.

**Angle:** We judge lives by what is visible.
