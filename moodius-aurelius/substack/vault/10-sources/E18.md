# Daniel Cox, Survey Center on American Life (2021): The friendship recession

**Finding:** Men reporting no close friends rose from 3% (1990) to 15% (2021); men with six or more close friends fell from 55% to 27%.

**Caveat:** US; comparison with 1990 uses a different survey.

**Angle:** In 1990, 3 in 100 men had no close friend. In 2021 it was 15.
