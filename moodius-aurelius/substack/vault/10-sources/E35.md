# James Marcia (after Erikson) (1966): Identity statuses

**Finding:** Identity forms through exploration and commitment; 'moratorium' (exploring, not yet committed) is a normal stage.

**Caveat:** Statuses are not fixed types.

**Angle:** Not knowing who you are at 27 may be exploration, not failure.
