# Webb & Sheeran (2006): The intention-behaviour gap

**Finding:** Interventions that produced a medium-to-large change in intention (d = 0.66) produced a small-to-medium change in behaviour (d = 0.36).

**Caveat:** Averages across health and other goals; habits moderate the effect.

**Angle:** Insight changes what you intend more than what you do.
