# Plato (c. 375 BCE): The Ring of Gyges

**Finding:** Glaucon: "No man would keep his hands off what was not his own when he could safely take what he liked out of the market."

**Caveat:** Glaucon states the cynic's case for Socrates to answer.

**Angle:** The first test is character; the second is courage.
