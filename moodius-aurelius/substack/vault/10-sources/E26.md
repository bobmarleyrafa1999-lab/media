# Dai, Milkman & Riis (2014): Fresh-start effect

**Finding:** Goal-seeking behaviour (gym visits, searches for 'diet') spikes after temporal landmarks like Mondays, birthdays and new months.

**Caveat:** Behavioural data, not outcomes.

**Angle:** Monday works because it feels like a new chapter. You can call any morning that.
