# van IJzendoorn (1995): Intergenerational transmission of attachment

**Finding:** Parental AAI matched infant secure/insecure attachment about 75% of the time; parental responsiveness explained only part ("transmission gap").

**Caveat:** Mechanism only partly understood; genes not separated.

**Angle:** Families pass things down, but not as a copy.
