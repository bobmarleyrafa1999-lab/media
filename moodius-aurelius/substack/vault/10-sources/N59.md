# Daryl Bem (1972): Self-perception theory

**Finding:** People often infer their own attitudes from observing their behaviour, especially when internal cues are weak or ambiguous.

**Caveat:** Competes with dissonance theory; applies to unclear attitudes.

**Angle:** You are the witness.
