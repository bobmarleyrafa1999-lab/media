# Brown, Nesse, Vinokur & Smith (2003): Giving support and mortality

**Finding:** Giving instrumental or emotional support was associated with lower mortality; receiving support, once giving was controlled, was not.

**Caveat:** Observational; cannot show causation.

**Angle:** Giving is good for you. Never receiving is the problem.
