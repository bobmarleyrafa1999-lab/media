# Wildschut, Sedikides, Arndt & Routledge (2006): Functions of nostalgia

**Finding:** Nostalgia is often triggered by low mood and loneliness, is mostly positive, and was found to bolster social bonds, raise self-regard and generate positive affect.

**Caveat:** Lab and student samples.

**Angle:** Missing who you were is often your mind reaching for the people who made you.
