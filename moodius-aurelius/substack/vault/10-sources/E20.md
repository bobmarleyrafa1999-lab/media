# Breines & Chen (2012): Self-compassion and improvement

**Finding:** After a failure or moral lapse, people induced to be self-compassionate were more motivated to improve (e.g. studied longer for a retest) than those given self-esteem boosts.

**Caveat:** Student samples, short-term outcomes.

**Angle:** Being harsh with yourself feels like discipline. The data says it's the kind voice that studies longer.
