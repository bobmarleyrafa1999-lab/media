# Seneca (c. 65 CE): Choose people who improve you

**Finding:** 'Associate with those who will make a better man of you. Welcome those whom you yourself can improve. The process is mutual; for men learn while they teach.'

**Caveat:** Wording confirmed against Goodreads text and the Latin 'Recede in te ipse...'; check Gummere print before scheduling.

**Angle:** Friendship as mutual training, not networking.
