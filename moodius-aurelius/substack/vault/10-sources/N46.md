# Kaufman & Zigler (1987): Is maltreatment passed down?

**Finding:** About one-third of people maltreated as children go on to maltreat their own, about six times the general rate; most do not. "Unqualified acceptance of the intergenerational hypothesis is unwarranted."

**Caveat:** Extreme case; estimates vary by design (retrospective studies inflate).

**Angle:** Inherited is not sentenced.
