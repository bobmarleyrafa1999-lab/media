# Mikulincer & Shaver (2007 (2nd ed. 2016)): Deactivating strategies

**Finding:** People high in avoidance tend to downplay attachment needs and suppress attachment-related thoughts (deactivation) to keep distance and self-reliance.

**Caveat:** Synthesis of many studies; describe tendencies, not individuals.

**Angle:** 'I'm fine on my own' can be a skill. It can also be a wall you built at 12.
