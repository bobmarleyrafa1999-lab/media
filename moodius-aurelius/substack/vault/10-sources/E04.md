# Ryan & Deci (2000): Self-determination theory

**Finding:** Wellbeing and durable motivation depend on three needs: autonomy, competence and relatedness. Autonomous motivation (you endorse the reason) outlasts controlled motivation (guilt, pressure, reward).

**Caveat:** Theory paper; effect sizes vary by study.

**Angle:** Same discipline, two engines: self-respect or self-punishment. Only one lasts.
