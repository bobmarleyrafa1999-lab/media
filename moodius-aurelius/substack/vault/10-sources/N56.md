# Powers, Koestner, Zuroff, Milyavskaya & Gorin (2011): Self-criticism and goal progress

**Finding:** Self-criticism predicted less goal progress (d+ = -0.54 across studies 1a-c; less weight loss over 6 months).

**Caveat:** Correlational.

**Angle:** The inner critic's promise doesn't hold.
