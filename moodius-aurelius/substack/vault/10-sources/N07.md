# Kross & Ayduk (2017): Self-distancing

**Finding:** Reflecting on a painful experience from a distanced perspective (e.g. third person, "fly on the wall") reduces emotional reactivity and rumination compared with immersed reflection.

**Caveat:** Mostly lab studies; effects vary by person.

**Angle:** Write about it as if it happened to someone you respect.
