# Newman, Duff & Baumeister (1997): Defensive projection via thought suppression

**Finding:** Proposes that suppressing thoughts about one's own undesirable traits makes those traits more accessible, so they colour how we see others.

**Caveat:** Lab studies; mechanism debated.

**Angle:** Pushing a trait out of mind can keep it in view, aimed at others.
