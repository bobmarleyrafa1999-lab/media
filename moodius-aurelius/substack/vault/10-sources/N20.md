# McAdams & McLean (2013): Narrative identity

**Finding:** People build identity through an evolving life story; redemptive sequences and exploratory processing are associated with wellbeing and growth.

**Caveat:** Correlational, mostly US.

**Angle:** You are partly the story you keep telling about yourself.
