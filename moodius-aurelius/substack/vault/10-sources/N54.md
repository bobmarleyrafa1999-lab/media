# Aristotle (c. 340 BCE): Wealth is for the sake of something else

**Finding:** "The life of money-making is one undertaken under compulsion, and wealth is evidently not the good we are seeking; for it is merely useful and for the sake of something else."

**Caveat:** First clause verified against Ross text; remainder from quote listings.
