# Nguyen, Ryan & Deci (2018): The deactivation effect

**Finding:** 15 minutes of device-free solitude lowered high-arousal affect and raised low-arousal affect; choosing thought content increased the calming effect; daily solitude reduced agitation over a week.

**Caveat:** Students; brief solitude, not isolation.

**Angle:** Solitude turns the volume down.
