# Whillans et al. (2017): Buying time promotes happiness

**Finding:** Spending money to save time was linked with greater life satisfaction across samples; a small field experiment found a similar effect.

**Caveat:** Correlational mostly.

**Angle:** The best thing money buys might be an hour.
