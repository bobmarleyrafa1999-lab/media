# Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle (2010): How long habits take

**Finding:** Median time to reach automaticity was about 66 days among participants with good model fit; estimates ranged from 18 to 254 days, and missing a day didn't derail it.

**Caveat:** 66 comes from a subset of 39; long estimates are model projections past 84 days.

**Angle:** It's not 21 days. It's closer to two months, and missing one day doesn't reset it.
