# Lieberman et al. (2007): Affect labelling

**Finding:** Labelling the emotion in a negative image reduced amygdala response compared with other ways of processing it, with more right ventrolateral prefrontal activity.

**Caveat:** Shows a neural association in a lab task, not that naming feelings fixes them.

**Angle:** Naming it is not weakness. It's the brake.
