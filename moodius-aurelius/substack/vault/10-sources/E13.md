# Wong, Ho, Wang & Miller (2017): Conformity to masculine norms and mental health

**Finding:** Conformity to masculine norms was modestly linked to worse mental health and moderately to less help-seeking. Self-reliance, playboy and power-over-women norms were the most consistently harmful; primacy of work was not significantly related.

**Caveat:** Correlational; not every 'masculine' trait is harmful.

**Angle:** Self-reliance is a virtue until it becomes the reason you never ask.
