# Wilson et al. (2014): People prefer mild shocks to thinking

**Finding:** Many participants found 6-15 minutes alone with their thoughts unpleasant, and many chose to give themselves a mild electric shock rather than sit idle.

**Caveat:** Student and community samples; short sessions.

**Angle:** We are not bored. We are avoiding something.
