# Crocker & Park (2004): The costly pursuit of self-esteem

**Finding:** Pursuing self-esteem by proving worth in contingent domains brings short-term boosts but costs in learning, relationships, autonomy and health.

**Caveat:** Theory review.

**Angle:** When your worth is on the line, every task becomes a trial.
