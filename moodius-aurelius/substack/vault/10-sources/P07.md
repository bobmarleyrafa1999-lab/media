# Albert Camus (1942): The absurd and revolt

**Finding:** The absurd is the clash between our demand for meaning and a silent world; the response is lucid revolt, not escape. 'One must imagine Sisyphus happy.'

**Caveat:** Camus is in copyright until 2031 in the EU; idea only, quote the famous line only as fair-use mention if at all.

**Angle:** Doing the same thing again tomorrow isn't failure. It can be defiance.
