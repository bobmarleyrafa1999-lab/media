# Kasser & Ryan (1996): Extrinsic vs intrinsic aspirations

**Finding:** Placing relatively high importance on wealth, fame and image (vs growth, relationships, community) was associated with lower wellbeing.

**Caveat:** Correlational; ambition itself isn't the issue.

**Angle:** Want more. Just know which 'more' you're chasing.
