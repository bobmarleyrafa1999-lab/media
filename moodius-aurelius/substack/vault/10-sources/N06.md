# Wegner, Schneider, Carter & White (1987): Paradoxical effects of thought suppression

**Finding:** Trying not to think of a white bear led to more thoughts of it afterwards (rebound).

**Caveat:** Effect size smaller in later meta-analyses.

**Angle:** Trying not to feel it is a way of rehearsing it.
