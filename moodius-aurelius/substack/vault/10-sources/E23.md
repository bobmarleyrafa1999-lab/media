# Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000): Spotlight effect

**Finding:** People overestimate how much others notice their appearance and actions (students in an embarrassing T-shirt guessed about half the room noticed; far fewer did).

**Caveat:** Use 'far fewer', not exact figures, unless checked.

**Angle:** Nobody is watching you as closely as you are.
