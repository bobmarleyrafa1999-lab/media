# Kumar & Epley (2018): Undervaluing gratitude

**Finding:** People writing gratitude letters underestimated how surprised and positive recipients would feel and overestimated how awkward they would feel.

**Caveat:** Lab and online samples.

**Angle:** The message you think is awkward is the one they keep.
