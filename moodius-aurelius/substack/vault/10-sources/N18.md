# Epley & Schroeder (2014): Mistakenly seeking solitude

**Finding:** Commuters asked to talk to a stranger had a more pleasant commute than those told to sit in solitude, though they predicted the opposite.

**Caveat:** Specific contexts.

**Angle:** We underestimate how much a small conversation will help.
