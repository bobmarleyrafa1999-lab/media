# Festinger & Carlsmith (1959): Insufficient justification

**Finding:** People paid $1 to call a boring task fun later rated it more enjoyable than people paid $20: small justification pushed attitudes to match behaviour.

**Caveat:** Classic study; mechanism debated.

**Angle:** The excuses you make for a job you hate get more convincing every year you stay.
