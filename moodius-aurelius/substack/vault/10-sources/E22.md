# Tangney & Dearing (2002): Shame vs guilt

**Finding:** Guilt ('I did a bad thing') tends to motivate repair; shame ('I am bad') tends to motivate hiding, denial or anger.

**Caveat:** Synthesis; cultures differ.

**Angle:** Guilt says fix it. Shame says hide. Learn which one is talking.
