# Aron, Melinat, Aron, Vallone & Bator (1997): Generating closeness

**Finding:** Strangers who took turns with escalating personal questions reported more closeness than small-talk pairs.

**Caveat:** Lab closeness, not lasting relationships.

**Angle:** Closeness comes from reciprocal disclosure.
