# Bushman (2002): Venting feeds the flame

**Finding:** Hitting a punching bag while ruminating about the provoker produced the most anger and aggression; doing nothing beat venting.

**Caveat:** Only rumination vs control differed significantly on aggression; student sample.

**Angle:** Venting rehearses anger.
