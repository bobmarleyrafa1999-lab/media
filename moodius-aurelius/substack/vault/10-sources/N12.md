# Maslach & Leiter (2016): Understanding the burnout experience

**Finding:** Burnout is a response to chronic job stressors with three dimensions: exhaustion, cynicism/depersonalisation and reduced efficacy; it is shaped by mismatches between person and job.

**Caveat:** Occupational phenomenon, not a medical diagnosis (WHO ICD-11).

**Angle:** Burnout is not a character flaw. It's a mismatch.
