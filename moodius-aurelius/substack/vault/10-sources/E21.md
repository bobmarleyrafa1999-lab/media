# Berglas & Jones (1978): Self-handicapping

**Finding:** People unsure about their ability created obstacles to performance, protecting their self-image if they failed.

**Caveat:** Classic lab paradigm.

**Angle:** If you never fully try, you never have to find out. That's the deal self-sabotage offers.
