# Killingsworth & Gilbert (2010): A wandering mind is an unhappy mind

**Finding:** People's minds wandered in a large share of sampled moments and they were less happy when wandering than when focused.

**Caveat:** App users self-selected; correlational.

**Angle:** Where your attention goes, your mood follows.
