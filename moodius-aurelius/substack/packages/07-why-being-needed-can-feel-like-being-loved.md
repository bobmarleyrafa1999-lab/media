## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. Psychologists distinguish caring about others, which tends to go with good outcomes, from caring for others to the exclusion of yourself, which goes with self-neglect and difficulty accepting help. The difference isn't how much you give. It's whether you're still in the equation. *(280 chars)*
2. Being needed is certain in a way love isn't. If people need you, you know where you stand. Love asks you to believe you'd still be wanted if you stopped being useful, and that can't be proven in advance. This week's essay is about the people who choose the certainty. *(267 chars)*
3. A small test: accept one offer of help this week that you'd normally turn down. Don't repay it the same day. Notice how strong the urge is. That urge is the whole essay. *(169 chars)*
