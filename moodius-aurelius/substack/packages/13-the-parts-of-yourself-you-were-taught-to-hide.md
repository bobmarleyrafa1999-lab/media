## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. In ten studies on secrets, people spent much more time thinking about what they were hiding when they were alone than actually hiding it from someone. And the thinking, not the hiding, went with lower wellbeing. Hiding costs most at 3 a.m. *(239 chars)*
2. Winnicott described a 'false self' a child builds to fit what the people around them need, protecting a true self that doesn't feel safe to show. He didn't see it as a failure. It's a good adaptation. It only becomes a problem when it's all there is. *(250 chars)*
3. Finish this sentence five times tonight: 'As a child, it wasn't safe to be…' Then ask which of those you'd like back. This week's essay is about the parts that went underground. *(177 chars)*
