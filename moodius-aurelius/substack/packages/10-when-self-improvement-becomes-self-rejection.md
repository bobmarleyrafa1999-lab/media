## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

3. A test for whether your self-improvement has turned into self-rejection: does a missed day feel like a missed day, or like evidence about who you are? This week's essay is about the difference. *(193 chars)*
