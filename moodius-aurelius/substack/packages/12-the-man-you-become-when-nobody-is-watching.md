## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. In 1984, students held their arms in cold water after being told that tolerance predicted a healthy heart. Those told 'longer is healthier' held on longer, and most denied changing their effort. We don't just watch ourselves. We perform for ourselves, and then miss it. *(269 chars)*
2. Marcus Aurelius, in a notebook nobody was meant to read: 'the soul is dyed by the thoughts.' Dye doesn't work in one dip. It works by repeated soaking. That's a fair description of how character is made. *(203 chars)*
3. This week's essay is about the man you become when nobody's watching. Not the big test. The washing-up at 11 p.m., the extra rep, the honest text. Most of a character is built there, and you're the only witness who's always present. *(232 chars)*
