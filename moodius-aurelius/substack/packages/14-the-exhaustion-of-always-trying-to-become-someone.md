## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. Byung-Chul Han argues we've moved from a society that told us what we couldn't do to one that tells us we can do anything, so we must. The result isn't freedom but a new exhaustion: people who overwork themselves voluntarily, in the name of becoming. *(250 chars)*
2. A test for whether your self-improvement is growth or a verdict: if you stopped improving tomorrow, would you be allowed to rest? If the answer is no, the project has quietly changed what it's for. *(197 chars)*
3. Make a list of everything you're currently trying to improve about yourself. Most people find ten or more. Keep two. Write 'finished, for now' next to the rest. See which ones you actually miss in a month. *(205 chars)*
