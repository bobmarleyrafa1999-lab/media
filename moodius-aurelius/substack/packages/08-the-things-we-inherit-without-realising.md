## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. A 1995 meta-analysis found that how parents talked about their own childhoods matched their babies' attachment patterns about 75% of the time. A strong link. But the obvious explanation, how responsive the parents were, accounted for only part of it. Researchers called the rest the 'transmission gap'. Something in between can change what's handed on. *(352 chars)*
2. Ezekiel quotes an old proverb: the fathers ate sour grapes and the children's teeth are set on edge. He quotes it to reject it. That tension, between what's handed down and the claim that we aren't bound by it, is where the research lands too. *(243 chars)*
3. Five words to write about tonight: anger, money, affection, conflict, failure. What did your house do with each? What do you do now? You'll find at least one you'd like to give back. *(182 chars)*
