Every group has one. The person who drives people to the airport, picks up at 2 a.m., and says "fine" when asked.

Helgeson and Fritz called one version of this unmitigated communion: caring for others to the exclusion of yourself. In their research it was linked to self-neglect, overinvolvement and difficulty accepting help. Ordinary caring wasn't.

Giving isn't the problem. Never receiving is.

Who looks after the one who looks after everyone?
