Understanding a pattern changes what you intend. It changes much less of what you do.

In a meta-analysis of 47 experiments, interventions that shifted people's intentions moved their actual behaviour about half as far. Insight is real. It just doesn't reach the moment on its own.

What's the one you keep repeating?
