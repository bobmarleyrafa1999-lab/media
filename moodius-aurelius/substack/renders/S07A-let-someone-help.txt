Giving is one of the best things a person can do. In a five-year study of older married couples, those who gave practical or emotional support were less likely to die during the study. It's observational, so it can't prove cause.

But there's a version of giving that keeps love out: always helping, never accepting. Eventually people stop offering.

This week, accept one offer you'd usually turn down. Which one will it be?

Evidence: Brown et al. (2003). Practice: Moodius. Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
