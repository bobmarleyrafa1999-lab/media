Plenty of men learned early that some feelings were welcome and some weren't. The joke, the change of subject, the sudden helpfulness: they arrive exactly when a feeling would have.

In meta-analyses, conforming to traditional masculine norms, self-reliance especially, was linked to worse mental health and less help-seeking.

Strength isn't hiding a feeling. It's being able to choose when to show it.
