A life can look right from outside and feel empty from inside.

In one study, 147 graduates who made progress on wealth, fame and image goals didn't report more wellbeing a year later, and showed more signs of stress. Those who made progress on relationships and personal growth did report more.

What would you keep if nobody could see your life from outside?

Evidence: Niemiec, Ryan & Deci (2009), a small, well-educated sample. Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
