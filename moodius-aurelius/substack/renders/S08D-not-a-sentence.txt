In the most studied and hardest case, whether people who were maltreated as children go on to maltreat their own, a 1987 review estimated about one in three did. That's a real risk. It also means most didn't.

The people who broke the pattern tended to have someone supportive, a partner or an adult in childhood, and an honest, coherent account of what had happened to them.

What pattern are you breaking?
