In a 2014 study, people sat alone in a room for 15 minutes with nothing to do but think, and a button that gave them a mild electric shock they'd earlier said they would pay to avoid. 67% of men and 25% of women pressed it at least once.

The lesson isn't that thinking is unbearable. It's that most of us are out of practice.

When did you last sit with your own thoughts?

Evidence: Wilson et al. (2014). Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
