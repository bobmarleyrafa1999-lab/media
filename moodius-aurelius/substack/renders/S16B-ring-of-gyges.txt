In Plato's Republic, Glaucon tells the story of a ring that makes its wearer invisible, and bets that anyone who had it would stop behaving well.

That's the usual test: what would you do wrong if nobody was watching? There's a second one: what would you finally do if nobody would ever know?

What would you try?
