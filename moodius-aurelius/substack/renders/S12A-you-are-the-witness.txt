Daryl Bem proposed that we often learn what we feel and value the same way other people do: by watching what we do and working backwards.

So the promises you keep to yourself in private do two things. They build a habit, and they give you evidence about who you are.

What evidence did you give yourself today?

Theory: Bem (1972), self-perception; it applies most when our sense of ourselves is unclear. Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
