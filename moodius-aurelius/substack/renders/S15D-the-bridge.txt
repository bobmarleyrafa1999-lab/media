In a 1974 study, men crossing a frightening suspension bridge were far more likely to call a woman they met there than men on a solid bridge. The usual reading: they mistook a pounding heart for attraction.

It's a small, old study and later results are mixed. But many people know the feeling: uncertainty and fear can feel exactly like love.

Was your most intense relationship also your most uncertain?
