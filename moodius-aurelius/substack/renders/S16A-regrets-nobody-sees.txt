Across six studies, people's regrets were mostly about their ideal selves, hopes and goals they didn't pursue (72%), rather than duties they didn't meet (28%). Asked for their single biggest regret, 76% named an ideal-self one.

Those are often the choices most shaped by an audience. Ask what you'd do if nobody would ever know.

What's the decision you're putting off?
