Most people who are hard on themselves think it's what keeps them going.

In four experiments, people nudged towards self-compassion about a weakness, a mistake or a failed test were more motivated to improve, not less.

Try this tonight: write to yourself about the thing you're trying to change, the way you'd write to a friend who was struggling with it. Honest about the problem, kind about the person.

What would that letter say?

Evidence: Breines & Chen (2012). Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
