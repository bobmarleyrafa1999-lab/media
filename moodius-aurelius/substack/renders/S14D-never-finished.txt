There's a tiredness that comes not from doing too much but from always being in progress.

Self-discrepancy theory links the gap between who we are and who we think we should be to distress. A self that's never finished keeps that gap open forever.

What would you stop improving if you were allowed to?

Theory: Higgins (1987); later studies support the general link, less so the specifics. Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
