Growth is a direction. Becoming, in the exhausting sense, is a verdict: who you are now isn't acceptable yet.

Seneca described people forever starting new plans, changing pursuits and places, and finding the restlessness came with them, because what they were escaping was themselves.

Which one is driving you?

Source: Seneca, On Tranquillity of Mind (paraphrased). Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
