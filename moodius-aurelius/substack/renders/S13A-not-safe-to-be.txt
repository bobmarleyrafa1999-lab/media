Every child learns which parts of them are welcome and which bring trouble. The rest go underground.

In ten studies on secrets, people spent far more time thinking about what they hid when they were alone than actually hiding it in conversation. It was the mind-wandering, not the hiding, that went with lower wellbeing.

As a child, what wasn't it safe to be?
