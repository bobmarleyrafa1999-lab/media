Fifteen minutes alone, no phone, no task. Students who did it felt fewer strong emotions afterwards, good and bad, and more calm. The effect was bigger when they chose what to think about. In a diary study, a daily 15 minutes made days less agitated.

Solitude isn't loneliness. It's a short gap in the noise.

Where's your 15 minutes going to be?
