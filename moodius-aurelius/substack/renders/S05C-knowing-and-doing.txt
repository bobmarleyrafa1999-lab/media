Socrates argued that nobody knowingly does wrong, so repeating a mistake must mean you don't truly understand it. Aristotle thought that was too neat: you can know something and still not have it active in the moment you act.

If Socrates is right, the fix is more understanding. If Aristotle is right, it's a plan for the moment.

Which sounds like you?

Source: Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics VII (paraphrased). Full essay on the Substack, link in bio.
