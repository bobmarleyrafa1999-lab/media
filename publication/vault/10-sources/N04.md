# Killingsworth, Kahneman & Mellers (2023): Income and emotional wellbeing: a conflict resolved

**Finding:** Happiness rises with log income for most people; a plateau appears only among the least happy group. For the happiest, the rise accelerates.

**Caveat:** US experience-sampling data; log scale means each doubling buys a similar step.

**Angle:** More money helps most people a little, on a log scale. It doesn't fix the miseries money can't touch.
