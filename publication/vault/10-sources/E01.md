# Hazan & Shaver (1987): Adult attachment

**Finding:** Adults' descriptions of their romantic relationships sorted into secure, avoidant and anxious/ambivalent patterns resembling infant attachment categories, and related to recalled relationships with parents.

**Caveat:** Self-report; the three-type model has since been replaced by two continuous dimensions (see E02). Don't type people.

**Angle:** Pulling away when someone gets close isn't coldness. It's often an old strategy that once worked.
