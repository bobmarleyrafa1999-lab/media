# Vogel, Rose, Roberts & Eckles (2014): Upward comparison on social media

**Finding:** Heavier Facebook use was associated with lower trait self-esteem, partly through more upward comparison; brief exposure to 'better' profiles lowered state self-esteem.

**Caveat:** Student samples; 2014 platforms.

**Angle:** Your feed is everyone's highlight reel against your behind-the-scenes. You knew that. Your nervous system didn't.
