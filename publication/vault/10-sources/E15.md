# Levant (1992): Normative male alexithymia

**Finding:** Proposes that boys socialised away from naming vulnerable feelings may grow into men with trouble putting emotions into words.

**Caveat:** Theory; the word 'alexithymia' is clinical, so never apply it to viewers.

**Angle:** Some men don't hide their feelings. They were never handed the words.
