# Schimel, Greenberg & Martens (2003): Defensive projection of a feared trait

**Finding:** People told they had a feared trait (repressed anger; dishonesty) rated an ambiguous other higher on that trait, and those allowed to project then showed less accessibility of / self-attribution of the trait.

**Caveat:** Two lab studies with students; not evidence for Jungian therapy.

**Angle:** The trait you can't stand in others may be doing a job for you.
