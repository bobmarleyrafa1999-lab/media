# Slepian, Chun & Mason (2017): The experience of secrecy

**Finding:** People currently held secrets in about 13 of 38 categories (about 5 never told); mind-wandering to secrets was more frequent than concealment and predicted lower wellbeing.

**Caveat:** Self-report; mostly online samples.

**Angle:** Hiding costs most at 3 a.m.
