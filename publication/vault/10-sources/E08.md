# Kashdan, Barrett & McKnight (2015): Emotional granularity

**Finding:** People who distinguish finely between negative emotions tend to regulate them better and are less likely to respond to stress with drinking or aggression.

**Caveat:** Mostly correlational.

**Angle:** 'Bad' isn't a feeling. Disappointed, embarrassed, lonely and tired are four different problems.
