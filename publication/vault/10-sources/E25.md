# Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006): Implementation intentions

**Finding:** 'If X, then I will Y' plans had a medium-to-large effect on goal attainment (d = 0.65).

**Caveat:** Effects vary by goal type.

**Angle:** Motivation is a mood. 'When I put the kettle on, I write one line' is a plan.
