# Jeffrey Hall (2019): Hours to make a friend

**Finding:** Moving from acquaintance to friend took roughly 50 hours together, and close friendship over 200.

**Caveat:** Estimates from adult samples; ranges wide.

**Angle:** Friendship isn't chemistry. It's hours. Most of us stop at ten.
