# Wong, Ho, Wang & Miller (2017): Conformity to masculine norms and mental health (meta-analysis)

**Finding:** Overall conformity had a modest unfavourable link with mental health and a moderate unfavourable link with help-seeking. Self-reliance, power over women and playboy norms were the most consistently harmful; primacy of work was not significantly related.

**Caveat:** Correlational; mostly US samples; norms differ in effect.

**Angle:** Of all the rules men learn, "handle it alone" is the one most tied to poor mental health.
