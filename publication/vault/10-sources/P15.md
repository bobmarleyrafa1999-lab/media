# bell hooks (2000): Love as action

**Finding:** hooks adopts M. Scott Peck's definition: love as the will to extend oneself to nurture one's own or another's growth, so love is a choice and an action.

**Caveat:** Idea only.

**Angle:** If it doesn't include care, respect and honesty, it isn't love, however strong it feels.
