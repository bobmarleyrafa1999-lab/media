# Simone de Beauvoir (1947): Ambiguity and freedom

**Finding:** Humans are ambiguous, both free subjects and objects; to will oneself free is also to will others free.

**Caveat:** Idea only.

**Angle:** Your freedom isn't real if it needs someone else to stay small.
