# Wood, Quinn & Kashy (2002): Habits in everyday life

**Finding:** 35% and 43% of recorded behaviours were habits; while performing them, thoughts were unrelated in about 60% and 51% of cases.

**Caveat:** Student samples; hourly self-report.

**Angle:** A large share of what you do runs while you think about something else.
