## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. The $75,000 happiness plateau became one of the most quoted numbers in psychology. In 2023 the two researchers who disagreed about it re-analysed the data together. For most people, there's no plateau. For the least happy, there is. The full story is this week's essay. *(269 chars)*
2. Seneca, Letters 2.6: "It is not the man who has too little, but the man who craves more, that is poor." Written by one of the richest men in Rome, which makes it harder to dismiss, not easier. *(192 chars)*
3. If the good feeling from your last big win lasted a week, that's not ingratitude. It's adaptation, and it's well documented. The useful question isn't how to keep the feeling. It's what the next goal is for. *(207 chars)*
