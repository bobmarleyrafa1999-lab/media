## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. A better question than "are you lonely?": if you weren't okay, who would know? Not who would care. Who would notice before you said anything. For many men the list is short, and they'd still say they're fine. *(208 chars)*
2. Jeffrey Hall's research estimated around 200 hours together to go from friend to close friend. That's an evening a week for a year. School scheduled those hours. Adult life doesn't. That's most of the male loneliness story, and the fix. *(236 chars)*
3. A 2017 meta-analysis of 78 samples found that of all masculine norms, self-reliance was among the few consistently linked to poorer mental health. It doesn't look like a vice. It looks like competence. That's why it's hard to see. *(230 chars)*
