## 6. Three Substack Notes (aim 250–300 characters; embed the post, don't link out)

1. 146 studies on writing about hard things. The honest result: it helps, on average, a little. That's not a reason to stop journaling. It's a reason to care about how you do it. This week's essay is about the how. *(211 chars)*
2. Marcus Aurelius rarely wrote "I" in his notebook. He wrote "thou", like a teacher talking to a student who keeps making the same mistake. Modern research on self-distancing suggests that's where some of the benefit lives. *(221 chars)*
3. If you've written about the same thing three times this month, you're not reflecting any more. You're rehearsing. Write the next page in the second person and see what changes. *(176 chars)*
